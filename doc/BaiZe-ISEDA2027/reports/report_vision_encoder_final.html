BaiZe Vision Encoder — Full-Line Experiment Summary (R2–R14)

Generated: 2026-10-08 · Agent: vision · Budget: ~498 GPU·h (8×H100-80G) · All numbers verified against EXPERIMENTS_VISION*.md and raw training logs

1. TL;DR

  • Architecture locked: OpenVision2 (pure-attention ViT, w512/d30/patch16/224², 126.8M params) — first on all four metrics in the R8 six-architecture comparison.
  • Contrastive ceiling broken: AIMv2-style dense objective (InfoNCE + masked-patch-MSE) lifts lp from 7.40% → 19.76% at 55.3M samples (R²=0.91, no saturation); 3-epoch extension reaches 20.27%.
  • Mask-ratio optimal = 0.6 (inverted-U, 5-arm ablation, lp=13.49% at 30k); contrast:patch weight-ratio 1:1 is best, patch-heavy direction is more tolerant.
  • InfoNCE prevents AR collapse: Pure causal AR without contrastive collapses at step 600 (C1=0.97); adding InfoNCE prevents collapse (C1≈0.48) but lp drops to 1.37% — causal AR is not viable at our data scale.
  • Bottleneck = data + objective, not architecture: Official OV2 L/14 (large-scale pretrained) scores 79.81% lp on the same protocol; our from-scratch best is 7.99% — a ~10× gap explained by 649× fewer samples.

4. Scaling Experiments (R9, R10, R11-G)

4.1 Data-side Scaling (R9)

OpenVision2 w512 (126.8M), 108k steps (55.3M samples). Power-law fit (R²≈0.94): acc = 0.251 − 0.864·N⁻⁰·⁰⁹⁰ → asymptote 25.1%. Reaching 20% needs ~6×10¹⁰ samples — ~525× beyond our ~118M.

4.2 Model-Size Scaling (R10)

2D (N,M) scaling: 5 widths (w384–w1024). M marginal effect is negative (≈ −2.2 to −2.4 lp pp per param doubling). w384 (71.5M) best lp = 7.99% @15.4M — smaller towers continuously better in data-limited regime.

4.3 AIMv2-style Breakthrough (R11-G)

Adding masked-patch-MSE dense term to InfoNCE breaks the 25.1% ceiling. Same architecture/data/schedule — only objective changes:

lp rises monotonically 11.50% → 19.76% at 55.3M (+12.4pp). R²=0.91, exponent c=0.040 (vs 0.090 contrastive) → saturates slower. 3-epoch extension reaches 20.27%, no saturation.

5. Ablation Experiments

5.1 Mask-Ratio (5 arms)

Only mask-ratio varies (0.3/0.5/0.6/0.75/0.9); w512, CC12M+Amshaker, 30k steps, InfoNCE+patch-MSE 1:1, frozen CLIP-768.

Conclusion: Inverted-U, peak at 0.6 (lp=13.49%). 0.3 and 0.75 within ±1.5pp noise; 0.5 (−1.58pp) and 0.9 (−1.96pp) significantly worse. 0.6 retained.

6. AIMv2 Paradigm Exploration (R11-G, R11-H, ④ Arm B/B-hybrid)

6.1 R11-G: AIMv2-style (bidirectional + InfoNCE + patch-MSE) — breakthrough

lp=19.76%@55.3M vs 7.40% baseline (+12.4pp). Both terms necessary (4-arm decomposition confirms neither alone suffices).

6.2 R11-H: Pure AR (bidirectional, no InfoNCE) — no collapse, no breakthrough

C1=0.43 (healthy), lp no breakthrough. Bidirectional attention is more robust to contrastive removal than causal.

6.3 ④ Arm B: Pure causal AR (no InfoNCE) — collapse @600

C1 rises 0.86 (step 300) → 0.97 (step 600), circuit-breaker triggers at 2% of training. AR next-patch admits trivial collapse.

6.4 ④ Arm B-hybrid: Causal AR + InfoNCE — no collapse, lp=1.37%

Adding InfoNCE prevents collapse (C1 0.30–0.55, 30k steps, no fuse). But lp@30k = 1.37% — 12.1pp drop vs baseline (13.49%). Zero-shot at chance (0.37%).

9. Implications for Formal Training

  1. Architecture: OpenVision2 w512 (126.8M, patch16/d30/224²). R8 confirmed pure-attention ViT optimal; R10 confirmed smaller towers better in data-limited regime.
  2. Objective: InfoNCE + masked-patch-MSE (1:1, mask-ratio 0.6). R11-G proved +12.4pp over contrastive. Ablations confirm 0.6 and 1:1 optimal.
  3. Data: Needs expansion. ~118M pairs → 7.99% lp; 25.1% contrastive asymptote; ~20%+ with dense objective at 176M samples. Data volume is the binding constraint.
  4. Epoch count > unique data: R12b (69.7M, 2ep, 18.81%) < R11-G (18.5M, 1ep, 19.76%) < 3-epoch (58.8M, 3ep, 20.27%). More epochs over existing data is more effective.
  5. Causal AR not viable at our scale: Pure AR collapses; hybrid AR+InfoNCE gives lp=1.37%. Only bidirectional + InfoNCE + dense works for our data budget.
  6. Text tower: Frozen CLIP-768 remains optimal. LoRA unfreezing is net negative.

10. Limitations and Honest Disclosure

  • Data-limited: ≤118M unique pairs (vs ~12B AIMv2). The 25.1% asymptote and ~20% ceiling are specific to this data budget.
  • Single seed: All runs use seed 1234. ±1.5pp noise band estimated from controlled reproductions, not multi-seed statistics.
  • Proxy metric: IN-1k frozen-trunk linear probe is primary. Retrieval proxy was degenerate (exactly chance), abandoned. Downstream multimodal task metrics not yet available.
  • Short captions: Our captions (~20 tok) are much shorter than AIMv2's LLaMA-3 synthetic captions. This specifically handicaps text-AR and caption-CE objectives.
  • Not multimodal aligned: Vision encoder evaluated standalone. Performance in full pipeline (Stage iv) may differ.
  • Architecture adaptations: R8's 6 architectures are self-developed, not official models. "SSM collapse" applies only to our recipe + adapted versions.
  • AIMv2-style is self-developed: Not official AIMv2 reproduction. Uses bidirectional (not causal), random masking (not prefix-LM), 1:1 weighting, ≤58.8M pairs (vs ~12B official).
