5. Corrected Conclusions

  1. SSM dtype: NO difference (1.00-1.01×) with warmup. "40% depression" retracted. 【实测】
  2. Hybrid 2.8-4.3× faster than dense at 128K-256K. Dense fails at 512K+. "Dense 3.3× faster" retracted. 【实测】
  3. Warmup is critical for SSM benchmarking (24× at 128K). All future benchmarks: WARMUP≥1.
  4. bf16 advantage = cache capacity only (382 vs 177 slots), not speed.
  5. Hybrid VRAM flat ~26-29GB (SSM O(1)) vs dense 27→35GB. Real advantage: long-ctx capacity.
