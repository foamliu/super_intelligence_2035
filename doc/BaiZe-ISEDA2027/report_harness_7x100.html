8. Conclusions and Limitations

8.1 Key Findings

  1. pi leads (40.9%) but most blocked (12/100, all sympy).
  2. cline/hermes/codex at 36-37.5% - similar at n=100.
  3. dsh weakest (26.6%) - underperforms even in fair scope.
  4. Repo difficulty dominates: django(39-61%) >> sympy(29-47%) >> others(0-17%).
  5. 22 blocked = parallel infra issue, not harness quality.
  6. 111 patch_applied=False masks non-django/sympy results.

8.2 Limitations

  • Small sample (100): 2-3% differences not significant.
  • Mixed modes: R1 serial vs R2 parallel vs codex serial-superset.
  • 22 blocked unresolved at report time (re-run in progress).
  • Eval env only reliable for django+sympy.
  • Single backbone (kimi-k2.6-cloud). No Docker (unshare sandbox).
  • Internal cross-eval, NOT SWE-bench leaderboard-comparable.

8.3 Recommendations for 7x300

  • Per-harness workdir isolation + rootfs flock.
  • Expand eval env to all 12 repos.
  • Test N=1->2->3 concurrency, verify block=0.
