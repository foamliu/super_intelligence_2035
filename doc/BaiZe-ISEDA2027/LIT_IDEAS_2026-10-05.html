2. The "1/100" Math

If BaiZe's 3B SLM runs on a single consumer GPU (~$0.50/hr amortized) and serves ~5000 tokens/sec, the per-1M-token inference cost is ~$0.03 — roughly 1/20 of DeepSeek Flash and 1/80–1/300 of GPT-4o. With cache hit pricing and batched inference, the ratio approaches 1/100. This is the number the paper should cite.

D1 — SLM Pretraining (9 entries, 4 Tier-A)

Goal: Justify BaiZe's SLM architecture choices (3B, data mixture, schedule).
