\section{Architecture and Pipeline Overview}\label{sec:overview}

BaiZe is an end-to-end multimodal agentic system assembled in five stages: (i) from-scratch pre-training of a hybrid language backbone, (ii) post-training of that backbone (instruction tuning and reinforcement learning to strengthen agentic capability), (iii) pre-training of a custom vision encoder, (iv) alignment of the two into a multimodal large language model, and (v) semiconductor-domain adaptation via reinforcement learning.
This section fixes the shared scaffolding---the two backbones, the overall pipeline, and the global training environment---before each stage is detailed in its own section.

\subsection{Language Backbone}\label{sec:arch}
The language backbone is a sequential hybrid model~\cite{dao2024transformers,nvidia2025nemotronh}: 56 transformer layers interleave Mamba-2 state-space layers, four grouped-query-attention (GQA) layers, and MLP layers.
The layer allocation follows the Nemotron-H sequential hybrid convention---four attention layers among the 56, the remainder Mamba-2 and MLP---as provided by Megatron-Core's Mamba-hybrid recipe, whose 56-layer pattern we reuse verbatim while halving only the hidden and FFN widths to reach the $\approx$2B scale.
BaiZe is not a Nemotron-H model: it is an order of magnitude smaller, contains no mixture-of-experts layers, and is pre-trained from scratch on an independent corpus under a fixed GPU-hour budget.
Each Mamba-2 layer uses 8 state-space groups; the four attention layers use 16 query heads and 4 query groups (GQA) with untied word embeddings.

\subsection{Vision Encoder}\label{sec:backbone-enc}
The vision encoder is pre-trained from scratch in stage~(iii) and is selected from six iso-parameter candidates at the $500$--$600$\,M scale; Section~\ref{sec:vision} reports the comparison and its outcome.

Table~\ref{tab:pipeline} summarises the five-stage pipeline.
