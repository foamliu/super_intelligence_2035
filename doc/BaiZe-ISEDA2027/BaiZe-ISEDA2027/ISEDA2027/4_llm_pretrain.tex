\section{LLM Pretraining}\label{sec:llm-pretrain}

Stage~(i) pre-trains the hybrid backbone (Section~\ref{sec:arch}) from scratch under a fixed budget.
% 中文：第 (i) 阶段在固定预算下从零预训练混合骨干（第 \ref{sec:arch} 节）。

\subsection{Architecture Selection}\label{sec:arch-rationale}
We adopt the hybrid backbone because a matched from-scratch comparison at $\approx$2B scale favours it over a dense transformer (MiniCPM5-2B~\cite{openbmb2026minicpm5}): lower loss with $11.6\%$ fewer parameters at $\sim$8\% throughput cost; the Megatron-Core provider (Section~\ref{sec:arch}) makes it executable without custom kernels.
% 中文：架构选型。采用混合骨干是因为约 2B 规模从零对比中混合模型优于稠密 Transformer（MiniCPM5-2B~\cite{openbmb2026minicpm5}）：少 $11.6\%$ 参数达更低损失，代价约 8\% 吞吐；Megatron-Core provider（第 \ref{sec:arch} 节）使其无需自定义核即可执行。

\subsection{Training Framework and Learning-Rate Schedule}\label{sec:framework}
We use NVIDIA NeMo~\cite{nvidia-nemo} with Megatron-Core's~\cite{nvidia-megatron} Mamba-hybrid provider (Section~\ref{sec:arch}).
% 中文：训练框架与学习率调度。我们用 NVIDIA NeMo~\cite{nvidia-nemo} 驱动 Megatron-Core~\cite{nvidia-megatron} 的 Mamba 混合 provider（第 \ref{sec:arch} 节）。

The model trains under a WSD schedule (LR $1\times10^{-3}$, $2{,}000$ warmup $=0.29\%$ of $700$k) at sequence $16{,}384$ ($613{,}992$ tok/s; $64\times$ extrapolation to $1$M). Decay has two segments: A anneals at 16K on instruction data ($60$--$70\%$), B extends on a \mbox{32K$\to$64K$\to$128K} ladder ($30$--$40\%$), with $lr_{\min}{=}1\times10^{-4}$.
% 中文：本模型在 WSD 调度下训练（LR $1\times10^{-3}$，预热 $2{,}000$ 步 $=700$k 的 $0.29\%$），序列 $16{,}384$（$613{,}992$ tok/s；到 $1$M 外推 $64\times$）。衰减分两段：A 段在 16K 上用指令数据退火（$60$–$70\%$），B 段沿 32K→64K→128K 阶梯扩展（$30$–$40\%$），$lr_{\min}{=}1\times10^{-4}$。

\subsection{Data}\label{sec:llm-data}
The pretraining data follows a three-stage curriculum: two stable stages (S1, S2; $700$k steps) and a decay annealing stage (Figure~\ref{fig:data-mix}).
% 中文：数据。预训练数据采用三阶段课程学习：两个稳定阶段（S1、S2；$700$k 步）加一个退火阶段（图 \ref{fig:data-mix}）。
\textbf{S1} ($0$--$350$k) uses a seven-source mixture yielding a $74{:}19{:}7$ web/code/math split, establishing a general-purpose language foundation.
% 中文：\textbf{S1}（$0$–$350$k）使用七源混合，产出 $74{:}19{:}7$ 的网页/代码/数学分布，建立通用语言基础。
\textbf{S2} ($350$k--$700$k) shifts to a $40{:}40{:}20$ web/code/math split to strengthen code and math capability.
% 中文：\textbf{S2}（$350$k–$700$k）切换为 $40{:}40{:}20$ 的网页/代码/数学配比，以增强代码与数学能力。
New sources are introduced in S2: stack-v3-train (L4-filtered, $430.7$B tokens; built on The Stack~v2~\cite{lozhkov2024stackv2}) for code and MegaMath ($\sim$371B tokens~\cite{liu2025megamath}), supplemented by Nemotron-CC-Math ($133$B~\cite{mahabadi2026nemotronccmath}) and FineMath~\cite{benallal2025smolm2}, for math.
% 中文：S2 引入新数据源：代码侧补入 stack-v3-train（L4 过滤后 $430.7$B token，基于 The Stack~v2~\cite{lozhkov2024stackv2}），数学侧补入 MegaMath（$\sim$371B token~\cite{liu2025megamath}）、Nemotron-CC-Math（$133$B~\cite{mahabadi2026nemotronccmath}）与 FineMath~\cite{benallal2025smolm2}。
These new sources are absent from S1's blend, so they have zero document-level overlap with S1; for the shared sources (Ultra-FineWeb, UltraData-Code, UltraData-Math), a different random seed is used in S2 to reduce document-level repetition.
% 中文：这些新源不在 S1 的 blend 中，因此与 S1 零文档重叠；对共享源（Ultra-FineWeb、UltraData-Code、UltraData-Math），S2 更换随机种子以降低文档级重复。
\textbf{Decay} ($700$k--$720$k) anneals toward instruction-SFT ($60\%$; e.g., UltraData-SFT~\cite{openbmb2026ultradata}, OpenMathInstruct-2~\cite{toshniwal2024openmathinstruct2}, NuminaMath~\cite{li2024numinamath}, OpenCodeReasoning~\cite{ahmad2025opencodereasoning}) and agentic-SFT ($12\%$; e.g., UltraData-SFT-Agent~\cite{openbmb2026ultradata}, AgentTrove~\cite{openthoughts2026agenttrove}), retaining a small general fraction (web $22\%$, math $4\%$, code $2\%$); segment~B extends context on a \mbox{32K$\to$64K$\to$128K} ladder ($30$--$40\%$).
% 中文：\textbf{Decay}（$700$k–$720$k）退火转向指令式 SFT（$60\%$；如 UltraData-SFT~\cite{openbmb2026ultradata}、OpenMathInstruct-2~\cite{toshniwal2024openmathinstruct2}、NuminaMath~\cite{li2024numinamath}、OpenCodeReasoning~\cite{ahmad2025opencodereasoning}）与智能体 SFT（$12\%$；如 UltraData-SFT-Agent~\cite{openbmb2026ultradata}、AgentTrove~\cite{openthoughts2026agenttrove}），保留少量通用成分（网页 $22\%$、数学 $4\%$、代码 $2\%$）；B 段在 32K→64K→128K 阶梯上扩展上下文（$30$–$40\%$）。
All sources are pre-blended into large shards ($\mathcal{O}(10)$ vs.\ $\mathcal{O}(600)$ index files per rank), bounding the data-loader footprint.
% 中文：各源预混合为大 shard（每 rank $\mathcal{O}(10)$ 个索引文件而非 $\mathcal{O}(600)$），限制数据加载内存占用。
Contamination is controlled by a $193{,}295$-entry 13-gram blacklist from $536$ benchmarks; shards are scanned for ${\geq}0.80$ n-gram overlap.
% 中文：污染控制由 $193{,}295$ 条 13-gram 黑名单实现（覆盖 $536$ 个基准）；shard 发布前扫描 ${\geq}0.80$ 的 n-gram 重叠。

\subsection{Long-Context Data Gate}\label{sec:longctx-data}
Only $\mathbf{2.52\%}$ of tokens sit in documents ${\geq}32$K and $0.03\%$ at ${\geq}128$K (mean $889$ tokens; Table~\ref{tab:longctx-data}), so we measured the distribution before budgeting segment~B.
% 中文：长上下文数据闸门。仅 $2.52\%$ 的 token 位于 ${\geq}32$K 的文档中，$0.03\%$ 达到 ${\geq}128$K（平均 $889$ token；表 \ref{tab:longctx-data}），因此在编列 B 段预算前测量了该分布。
We gate the ladder: ${\geq}32$K share must reach $20\%$ and ${\geq}128$K $5\%$ (else it stops at 64K), with EOD resets and de-duplication.
% 中文：我们设闸门：${\geq}32$K 占比须达 $20\%$、${\geq}128$K 达 $5\%$（否则止于 64K），并对 EOD 重置状态与去重。
Long-context training data is drawn from documents ${\geq}32$K within the general web corpus, supplemented by publicly available long-context collections such as ProLong~\cite{gao2024prolong}, LongAlign~\cite{bai2024longalign}, and Whole-Books~\cite{ysngkil2026wholebooks}; retrieval quality is evaluated with RULER~\cite{hsieh2024ruler}.
% 中文：长上下文训练数据取自通用语料中 ${\geq}32$K 的文档，并辅以公开长上下文数据集如 ProLong~\cite{gao2024prolong}、LongAlign~\cite{bai2024longalign} 与 Whole-Books~\cite{ysngkil2026wholebooks}；检索质量用 RULER~\cite{hsieh2024ruler} 评估。

\subsection{Results and Production Run}\label{sec:llm-results}
The model uses WSD at LR $1\times10^{-3}$ with a two-stage stable curriculum ($74{:}19{:}7$ $\to$ $40{:}40{:}20$) followed by a $60{:}12{:}28$ annealing mix (instruction-SFT / agentic-SFT / general).
% 中文：结果与生产训练。本模型采用 WSD、LR $1\times10^{-3}$，稳定段两阶段课程（$74{:}19{:}7$ $\to$ $40{:}40{:}20$），退火混合 $60{:}12{:}28$（指令 SFT / 智能体 SFT / 通用）。


The recipe is scaled to $2.94$T tokens at $\texttt{DP}{=}16$ over two $8{\times}$H100 nodes, $\texttt{GBS}{=}256$ at sequence $16{,}384$ ($4{,}194{,}304$ tokens/step), FP8 tensorwise, AdamW.
% 中文：该配方被放大到 $2.94$T token：跨两个 $8{\times}$H100 节点，$\texttt{DP}{=}16$，$\texttt{GBS}{=}256$、序列 $16{,}384$（每步 $4{,}194{,}304$ token），FP8 tensorwise，AdamW。
At the time of writing (2026-11-13), the run has passed iteration $190{,}000$ of $700{,}000$ ($27.1\%$) at $\mathbf{\approx}598$K tok/s, training loss $\approx2.03$, with zero NaN or skipped iterations (Table~\ref{tab:prod-budget}). The $613{,}992$ tok/s peak is FP8 at $16{,}384$; shorter sequences use BF16.
% 中文：截至撰文时（2026-11-13），运行已到 $700{,}000$ 步中的第 $190{,}000$ 步（$27.1\%$），约 $\mathbf{598}$K tok/s，训练损失约 $2.03$，无 NaN 或跳过迭代（表 \ref{tab:prod-budget}）。$613{,}992$ tok/s 峰值为 $16{,}384$ 时的 FP8 测量值；更短序列用 BF16。

\subsection{Long-Context Training in the Decay Tail}\label{sec:longctx-train}
Long-context training is segment~B of the decay: the LR is already low, and neither SFT nor RL builds retrieval ability at this scale.
% 中文：衰减尾部中的长上下文训练。长上下文训练是衰减的 B 段：LR 已低，SFT 与 RL 都不建立该量级的检索能力。
The decay budget is ${\sim}2.9\%$ of main volume ($83.9$B / $20{,}000$ steps); long-context data is $30$--$40\%$ ($25$--$34$B). The ladder runs \mbox{32K$\to$64K$\to$128K} at $\texttt{CP}\in\{2,4,8\}$, $16{,}384$ tokens/GPU, continuing decay without re-warming; $\theta{=}10^{6}$ fixed, so 128K is trained and 1M is $8\times$ extrapolation (Table~\ref{tab:longctx-train}). Reference cost ${\sim}880$ GPU-hours (Table~\ref{tab:prod-budget}).
% 中文：衰减预算为主训练量的 ${\sim}2.9\%$（$83.9$B / $20{,}000$ 步）；长上下文数据占 $30$–$40\%$（$25$–$34$B）。阶梯按 32K→64K→128K 运行，$\texttt{CP}\in\{2,4,8\}$，每 GPU $16{,}384$ token，延续衰减不重预热；$\theta{=}10^{6}$ 固定，故 128K 由训练达成、1M 为 $8\times$ 外推（表 \ref{tab:longctx-train}）。参考成本约 $880$ GPU·h（表 \ref{tab:prod-budget}）。
At WSD end: one snapshot---needle retrieval (passkey + RULER~\cite{hsieh2024ruler}, target ${\geq}95\%$ at 1M) and perplexity extrapolation ($\leq 1.10\times$).
% 中文：WSD 结束时：一个快照——针检索（passkey + RULER，1M 目标 ${\geq}95\%$）与困惑度外推（$\leq 1.10\times$）。

\subsection{Long-Context Capability and Inference Cost}\label{sec:longctx}
With $\theta{=}10^{6}$ on the four attention layers, perplexity evolves $83.2\to51.5\to53.3\to53.0\to55.8\to55.4$ from 4K to 1M, flat beyond 64K; only $4/56{=}7.1\%$ of layers carry RoPE. Under SGLang, the hybrid achieves $39{,}377$ tok/s prefill at $128$K and serves $1$M in $172$s at $34.7$\,GB; the dense transformer cannot serve beyond $512$K on a single $80$\,GB H100 (KV-pool cap ${\approx}455$K). The advantage is architectural: $O(1)$ per-layer state for $52$ of $56$ layers. While profiling the hybrid under a CPU-only \texttt{llama.cpp} backend, we isolated and fixed an upstream inference-correctness bug for \texttt{Nemotron-H} (missing RoPE on attention layers and a wrong FFN activation), contributed as a fix to llama.cpp\footnote{\url{https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/31703}}; the fix restores 1-token logits to $\Delta_{\mathrm{max}}{=}0.006$ against the HF reference.
% 中文：长上下文能力与推理成本。在 4 个注意力层上采用 $\theta{=}10^{6}$ 后，困惑度从 4K 到 1M 依次为 $83.2\to51.5\to53.3\to53.0\to55.8\to55.4$，64K 后基本持平；仅 $4/56{=}7.1\%$ 的层携带 RoPE。在 SGLang 下，混合模型 $128$K 时 prefill 达 $39{,}377$ tok/s，1M 时以 $172$ 秒、$34.7$ GB 完成；稠密 Transformer 在单张 80 GB H100 上无法服务超过 $512$K（KV 池上限约 $455$K）。优势是架构性的：56 层中 52 层的逐层状态以 $O(1)$ 增长。
