\section{LLM Pretraining}\label{sec:llm-pretrain}

Stage~(i) pre-trains the hybrid backbone (Section~\ref{sec:arch}) from scratch under a fixed budget.
% 中文：第 (i) 阶段在固定预算下从零预训练混合骨干（第 \ref{sec:arch} 节）。

\subsection{Architecture Selection}\label{sec:arch-rationale}
We adopt the hybrid backbone because a matched from-scratch comparison at $\approx$2B scale favours it over a dense transformer (MiniCPM5-2B~\cite{openbmb2026minicpm5}): lower loss with $11.6\%$ fewer parameters ($\approx$2.22B vs.\ 2.51B) at $\sim$8\% throughput cost (Table~\ref{tab:archcomp}); the Megatron-Core provider (Section~\ref{sec:arch}) makes it executable without custom kernels.
% 中文：架构选型。采用混合骨干是因为约 2B 规模从零对比中混合模型优于稠密 Transformer（MiniCPM5-2B~\cite{openbmb2026minicpm5}）：少 $11.6\%$ 参数（约 2.22B 对 2.51B）达更低损失，代价约 8% 吞吐（表 \ref{tab:archcomp}）；Megatron-Core provider（第 \ref{sec:arch} 节）使其无需自定义核即可执行。

\subsection{Training Framework and Learning-Rate Schedule}\label{sec:framework}
We use NVIDIA NeMo~\cite{nvidia-nemo} with Megatron-Core's~\cite{nvidia-megatron} Mamba-hybrid provider (Section~\ref{sec:arch}); hyper-parameter searches run at a reduced iteration count with short-horizon ranking transferred to full scale.
% 中文：训练框架与学习率调度。我们用 NVIDIA NeMo~\cite{nvidia-nemo} 驱动 Megatron-Core~\cite{nvidia-megatron} 的 Mamba 混合 provider（第 \ref{sec:arch} 节）；超参搜索以缩减迭代数运行，短程排序迁移到全规模。

BaiZe trains under a WSD schedule (LR $1\times10^{-3}$, $2{,}000$ warmup $=0.29\%$ of $700$k; search found $250$ optimal at $5$k but the proportion narrows at full scale) at sequence $16{,}384$ ($613{,}992$ tok/s; $64\times$ extrapolation to $1$M). Decay has two segments: A anneals at 16K on instruction data ($60$--$70\%$), B extends on a \mbox{32K$\to$64K$\to$128K} ladder ($30$--$40\%$), with $lr_{\min}{=}1\times10^{-4}$.
% 中文：BaiZe 在 WSD 调度下训练（LR $1\times10^{-3}$，预热 $2{,}000$ 步 $=700$k 的 $0.29\%$；搜索在 $5$k 步上发现 $250$ 最优但全规模下比例收窄），序列 $16{,}384$（$613{,}992$ tok/s；到 $1$M 外推 $64\times$）。衰减分两段：A 段在 16K 上用指令数据退火（$60$–$70\%$），B 段沿 32K→64K→128K 阶梯扩展（$30$–$40\%$），$lr_{\min}{=}1\times10^{-4}$。

\subsection{Search Protocol}\label{sec:search}
The recipe was selected via a staged single-variable protocol at $5000$ steps: S1 baseline; S2 LR sweep ($2\times10^{-4}$--$1\times10^{-3}$, seven points); S3 schedule axes (WSD vs.\ cosine, decay ratio, warmup, $min\_lr$); S4 annealing mix; S5 long run + three seeds. Total budget $\approx$82 GPU-hours on eight H100s; losses are proxy measurements for ranking.
% 中文：搜索协议。配方以分阶段单变量协议在 $5000$ 步上选出：S1 基线；S2 学习率扫描（$2\times10^{-4}$–$1\times10^{-3}$，七点）；S3 调度轴（WSD 与 cosine、衰减比例、预热、$min\_lr$）；S4 退火配比；S5 长跑加三种子。总预算约 $82$ GPU·h（八张 H100）；损失为排序用代理测量。

\subsection{Data}\label{sec:llm-data}
The stable phase uses a seven-source mixture yielding a $74{:}19{:}7$ web/code/math split ($0.74$ \texttt{Ultra-FineWeb}, $0.19$ \texttt{UltraData-Code}, $0.07$ \texttt{UltraData-Math}); a $400$-trial Bayesian search confirmed this region optimal within $0.6$\,pp.
% 中文：数据。稳定阶段使用七源混合，产出 $74{:}19{:}7$ 的网页/代码/数学分布（$0.74$ \texttt{Ultra-FineWeb}、$0.19$ \texttt{UltraData-Code}、$0.07$ \texttt{UltraData-Math}）；$400$ 次贝叶斯搜索在 $0.6$ pp 内确认该区域最优。
Sources are pre-blended into large shards ($\mathcal{O}(10)$ vs.\ $\mathcal{O}(600)$ index files per rank), bounding the data-loader footprint.
% 中文：各源预混合为大 shard（每 rank $\mathcal{O}(10)$ 个索引文件而非 $\mathcal{O}(600)$），限制数据加载内存占用。
Contamination is controlled by a $193{,}295$-entry 13-gram blacklist from $536$ benchmarks; shards are scanned for ${\geq}0.80$ n-gram overlap.
% 中文：污染控制由 $193{,}295$ 条 13-gram 黑名单实现（覆盖 $536$ 个基准）；shard 发布前扫描 ${\geq}0.80$ 的 n-gram 重叠。

\subsection{Long-Context Data Gate}\label{sec:longctx-data}
Only $\mathbf{2.52\%}$ of tokens sit in documents ${\geq}32$K and $0.03\%$ at ${\geq}128$K (mean $889$ tokens; Table~\ref{tab:longctx-data}), so we measured the distribution before budgeting segment~B.
% 中文：长上下文数据闸门。仅 $2.52\%$ 的 token 位于 ${\geq}32$K 的文档中，$0.03\%$ 达到 ${\geq}128$K（平均 $889$ token；表 \ref{tab:longctx-data}），因此在编列 B 段预算前测量了该分布。
We gate the ladder: ${\geq}32$K share must reach $20\%$ and ${\geq}128$K $5\%$ (else it stops at 64K), with EOD resets and de-duplication.

\subsection{Results and Production Run}\label{sec:llm-results}
The search selects WSD at LR $1\times10^{-3}$ with $86{:}10{:}4$ annealing, reaching validation loss $\mathbf{2.2054}$ at $20000$ steps ($2.6739\pm0.0469$ across three seeds at $5000$; Table~\ref{tab:configs}).
% 中文：结果与生产训练。搜索选出 WSD 下 LR $1\times10^{-3}$、退火混合 $86{:}10{:}4$，20000 步验证损失 $\mathbf{2.2054}$（5000 步三种子 $2.6739\pm0.0469$；表 \ref{tab:configs}）。


The recipe is scaled to $2.94$T tokens at $\texttt{DP}{=}16$ over two $8{\times}$H100 nodes, $\texttt{GBS}{=}256$ at sequence $16{,}384$ ($4{,}194{,}304$ tokens/step), FP8 tensorwise, AdamW.
% 中文：该配方被放大到 $2.94$T token：跨两个 $8{\times}$H100 节点，$\texttt{DP}{=}16$，$\texttt{GBS}{=}256$、序列 $16{,}384$（每步 $4{,}194{,}304$ token），FP8 tensorwise，AdamW。
At the time of writing, the run has passed iteration $30{,}660$ of $700{,}000$ at $\mathbf{\approx}598$K tok/s, training loss $2.151$, no NaN (Table~\ref{tab:prod-budget}). The $613{,}992$ tok/s peak is FP8 at $16{,}384$; shorter sequences use BF16.
% 中文：截至撰文时，运行已到 $700{,}000$ 步中的第 $30{,}660$ 步，约 $\mathbf{598}$K tok/s，训练损失 $2.151$，无 NaN（表 \ref{tab:prod-budget}）。$613{,}992$ tok/s 峰值为 $16{,}384$ 时的 FP8 测量值；更短序列用 BF16。

\subsection{Long-Context Training in the Decay Tail}\label{sec:longctx-train}
Long-context training is segment~B of the decay: the LR is already low, and neither SFT nor RL builds retrieval ability at this scale.
% 中文：衰减尾部中的长上下文训练。长上下文训练是衰减的 B 段：LR 已低，SFT 与 RL 都不建立该量级的检索能力。
The decay budget is ${\sim}2.9\%$ of main volume ($83.9$B / $20{,}000$ steps); long-context data is $30$--$40\%$ ($25$--$34$B). The ladder runs \mbox{32K$\to$64K$\to$128K} at $\texttt{CP}\in\{2,4,8\}$, $16{,}384$ tokens/GPU, continuing decay without re-warming; $\theta{=}10^{6}$ fixed, so 128K is trained and 1M is $8\times$ extrapolation (Table~\ref{tab:longctx-train}). Reference cost ${\sim}880$ GPU-hours (Table~\ref{tab:prod-budget}).
% 中文：衰减预算为主训练量的 ${\sim}2.9\%$（$83.9$B / $20{,}000$ 步）；长上下文数据占 $30$–$40\%$（$25$–$34$B）。阶梯按 32K→64K→128K 运行，$\texttt{CP}\in\{2,4,8\}$，每 GPU $16{,}384$ token，延续衰减不重预热；$\theta{=}10^{6}$ 固定，故 128K 由训练达成、1M 为 $8\times$ 外推（表 \ref{tab:longctx-train}）。参考成本约 $880$ GPU·h（表 \ref{tab:prod-budget}）。
At WSD end: one snapshot---needle retrieval (passkey + RULER, target ${\geq}95\%$ at 1M) and perplexity extrapolation ($\leq 1.10\times$).
% 中文：WSD 结束时：一个快照——针检索（passkey + RULER，1M 目标 ${\geq}95\%$）与困惑度外推（$\leq 1.10\times$）。

\subsection{Long-Context Capability and Inference Cost}\label{sec:longctx}
With $\theta{=}10^{6}$ on the four attention layers, perplexity evolves $83.2\to51.5\to53.3\to53.0\to55.8\to55.4$ from 4K to 1M, flat beyond 64K; only $4/56{=}7.1\%$ of layers carry RoPE. Under SGLang, the hybrid achieves $39{,}377$ tok/s prefill at $128$K and serves $1$M in $172$s at $34.7$\,GB; the dense transformer cannot serve beyond $512$K on a single $80$\,GB H100 (KV-pool cap ${\approx}455$K). The advantage is architectural: $O(1)$ per-layer state for $52$ of $56$ layers.
% 中文：长上下文能力与推理成本。在 4 个注意力层上采用 $\theta{=}10^{6}$ 后，困惑度从 4K 到 1M 依次为 $83.2\to51.5\to53.3\to53.0\to55.8\to55.4$，64K 后基本持平；仅 $4/56{=}7.1\%$ 的层携带 RoPE。在 SGLang 下，混合模型 $128$K 时 prefill 达 $39{,}377$ tok/s，1M 时以 $172$ 秒、$34.7$ GB 完成；稠密 Transformer 在单张 80 GB H100 上无法服务超过 $512$K（KV 池上限约 $455$K）。优势是架构性的：56 层中 52 层的逐层状态以 $O(1)$ 增长。
