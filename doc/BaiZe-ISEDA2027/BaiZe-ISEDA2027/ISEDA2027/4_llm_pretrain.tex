\section{LLM Pretraining}\label{sec:llm-pretrain}

Stage~(i) pre-trains the hybrid language backbone (Section~\ref{sec:arch}) from scratch under a fixed budget.
% 中文：第 (i) 阶段在固定预算下从零预训练混合语言骨干（第 \ref{sec:arch} 节）。
This section covers the architecture-selection rationale, the data, the production recipe---organised as a warmup--stable--decay (WSD) schedule whose decay phase carries two segments, a \emph{short-context annealing segment} and a \emph{long-context extension segment} (Section~\ref{sec:longctx-train})---and the resulting measurements; the backbone configuration itself is given in Table~\ref{tab:config}.
% 中文：本节涵盖架构选型的理由、数据、生产配方——按预热—稳定—衰减（WSD）调度组织，其衰减阶段包含两个子段：\emph{短上下文退火段} 与 \emph{长上下文扩展段}（第 \ref{sec:longctx-train} 节）——以及由此得到的测量结果；骨干配置本身见表 \ref{tab:config}。

\subsection{Architecture Selection Rationale}\label{sec:arch-rationale}
We adopt the hybrid backbone on three grounds.
% 中文：我们基于三点理由采用混合骨干。
\textbf{(i) Inference efficiency.} State-space layers decode with constant-time recurrence per token, while the sparse attention layers retain long-range context; this directly targets the inference-heavy, cost-sensitive deployments that motivate BaiZe.
\textbf{(ii) Controlled comparison.} A matched from-scratch comparison at the $\approx$2B scale between a dense transformer (MiniCPM5-2B~\cite{openbmb2026minicpm5}) and Mamba2-hybrid---trained under identical data, seed, and GPU footprint---favours the hybrid, which converges to a lower loss with fewer parameters ($\approx$2.22B vs.\ 2.51B, $-11.6\%$) and decodes faster under our direct-drive harness, at a $\sim$8\% training-throughput cost (Table~\ref{tab:archcomp}). Across all five checkpoints from 500 to 5000 steps, the hybrid consistently attains lower loss than the dense baseline, with the gap widening to $\Delta{\approx}0.30$ by step 5000---confirming the advantage is not an early-training artifact and comes with an 11.6\% parameter reduction.
\textbf{(iii) Feasibility.} Megatron-Core ships a Mamba-hybrid provider whose 56-layer interleaving pattern we reuse verbatim, halving only the hidden and FFN widths to reach the $\approx$2B scale, making the backbone directly executable under the NeMo recipe without custom kernels.

\subsection{Training Framework and Reduced-Horizon Search}\label{sec:framework}
We train with the NVIDIA NeMo~\cite{nvidia-nemo} pre-training entry point, driving the Megatron-Core~\cite{nvidia-megatron} \texttt{NVIDIAMambaHybridModelProvider2B} provider directly with $\texttt{tensor\_model\_parallel\_size}=1$.
% 中文：我们采用 NVIDIA NeMo \cite{nvidia-nemo} 的预训练入口，以 $\texttt{tensor\_model\_parallel\_size}=1$ 直接驱动 Megatron-Core \cite{nvidia-megatron} 的 \texttt{NVIDIAMambaHybridModelProvider2B} provider。
To keep the entire sweep within budget, we run all hyper-parameter searches at a reduced iteration count and treat the resulting short-horizon ranking as predictive of the full-model optimum.
% 中文：为使整个扫描都在预算内，我们以缩减的迭代数运行全部超参搜索，并把由此得到的短程排序视为对全模型最优点的预测。
Fused kernels, selective activation recomputation, and cross-entropy loss fusion are enabled to raise throughput.
% 中文：为提升吞吐，启用融合核、选择性激活重计算与交叉熵损失融合。

\subsection{Learning-Rate Schedule: WSD with a Two-Segment Decay}\label{sec:wsd}
BaiZe trains under a warmup--stable--decay (WSD) schedule with a stable learning rate of $1\times10^{-3}$ and $2{,}000$ warmup steps (Table~\ref{tab:wsd}).
% 中文：BaiZe 在预热—稳定—衰减（WSD）调度下训练，稳定学习率为 $1\times10^{-3}$，预热 $2{,}000$ 步（表 \ref{tab:wsd}）。
The schedule has three segments, and the organisation of the third is the subject of Section~\ref{sec:longctx-train}:
% 中文：该调度包含三个段，第三段的组织方式正是第 \ref{sec:longctx-train} 节的主题：
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Warmup + stable} ($700{,}000$ steps, $4.19$M tokens/step, $2.94$T tokens; Table~\ref{tab:wsd}). The learning rate is held at its peak. The main training sequence length is $16{,}384$; this is a deliberate choice, not a limitation: 16K is the throughput-optimal operating point of the backbone on our cluster ($613{,}992$ tok/s at $\texttt{CP}{=}1$/$\texttt{DP}{=}16$ with computing-graph replay off; measured), and it keeps the extrapolation ratio to the $1$M product target at $64\times$ instead of the $256\times$ that a 4K pretraining length would imply.
\item \textbf{Decay---segment~A, short-context annealing} ($60$--$70\%$ of the decay budget). The learning rate enters its decay while the sequence length stays at 16K, annealing the model on the high-quality instruction and curated-web mixture.
\item \textbf{Decay---segment~B, long-context extension} ($30$--$40\%$ of the decay budget). The sequence length is extended on a $32$K$\to$64K$\to$128K ladder while the decayed learning rate continues downward (Section~\ref{sec:longctx-train}).
\end{itemize}
The decay segment itself follows $lr(p)=lr_{\text{peak}}\,(lr_{\min}/lr_{\text{peak}})^{p}$ with $p\in[0,1]$ (exponential) and $lr_{\min}{=}1\times10^{-4}$, the setting retained by the schedule-family ablation of Section~\ref{sec:llm-results}; the per-step values it produces ($p{=}0.25/0.5/0.75\Rightarrow5.62/3.16/1.78\times10^{-4}$) are the learning rates under which segment~B runs.
% 中文：衰减段本身遵循 $lr(p)=lr_{\text{peak}}\,(lr_{\min}/lr_{\text{peak}})^{p}$（$p\in[0,1]$，指数式），$lr_{\min}{=}1\times10^{-4}$；这是调度族消融所保留的设定（第 \ref{sec:llm-results} 节）。它产生的逐步取值（$p{=}0.25/0.5/0.75\Rightarrow5.62/3.16/1.78\times10^{-4}$）正是 B 段运行时的学习率。

\subsection{Search Protocol Preceding the Production Run}\label{sec:search}
Because a full grid is infeasible under a fixed GPU budget, the production recipe was selected with a staged, single-variable-at-a-time protocol run on the same backbone at a reduced horizon:
% 中文：由于在固定 GPU 预算下完整网格不可行，生产配方是在同一骨干上以缩减步长运行分阶段的、单变量逐一变化的协议选出的：
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{S1 (baseline).} Reproduce the Mamba2-hybrid reference configuration (stable LR $3\times10^{-4}$, WSD) to establish a loss/throughput baseline.
\item \textbf{S2 (learning-rate sweep).} Sweep the stable learning rate over seven points from $2\times10^{-4}$ to $1\times10^{-3}$ with WSD held fixed.
\item \textbf{S3 (schedule axes).} On the winning learning rate, perturb one variable at a time---schedule family (WSD vs.\ cosine), decay ratio and decay shape, warmup length, and $min\_lr$.
\item \textbf{S4 (annealing data mix).} Ablate the annealing data blend (pure Ultra-FineWeb-L3 vs.\ adding code and math) under the winning configuration.
\item \textbf{S5 (long run + seeds).} Retrain the winning configuration from scratch over a $20000$-step horizon, plus a three-seed short-horizon reproducibility check.
\end{itemize}
These sweeps are \emph{proxy experiments}: their hyper-parameter ranking is transferred to the production scale, and the absolute losses they report are not production estimates.
% 中文：这些扫描是 \emph{代理实验}：其超参排序被迁移到生产规模，它们报告的绝对损失不是生产估计。
The production run is specified by the budget of Table~\ref{tab:wsd} and is reported in Section~\ref{sec:full-train}; the long-context segment of that budget is specified in Section~\ref{sec:longctx-train}.
% 中文：生产训练由表 \ref{tab:wsd} 的预算规定，并在第 \ref{sec:full-train} 节报告；该预算中的长上下文子段在第 \ref{sec:longctx-train} 节给出规格。

At every stage only a single variable changes relative to the running best, isolating each factor's contribution while keeping total GPU-hours within budget.
% 中文：每个阶段相对于当前最优只改变单一变量，从而隔离各因素的贡献，同时把总 GPU·h 控制在预算内。
The architectural search space is validated up front: the hybrid (Mamba2-hybrid) and dense (MiniCPM5-2B) candidates are compared head-to-head from scratch (Section~\ref{sec:arch-rationale}), which selects the hybrid backbone before the hyper-parameter search begins.
% 中文：架构搜索空间被预先验证：混合（Mamba2-hybrid）与稠密（MiniCPM5-2B）候选从头同等条件对比，使后续的超参搜索只需在一族架构内进行。

\subsection{Data}\label{sec:llm-data}
Pre-training uses the \texttt{Ultra-FineWeb-L3} English subset ($\approx$690\,B tokens across 616 parquet shards, 618\,GiB), streamed and packed, tokenised with the DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash tokenizer~\cite{deepseek2024tokenizer} (vocabulary size $129{,}281$).
% 中文：预训练使用 \texttt{Ultra-FineWeb-L3} 英文子集（约 690 B token，分布于 616 个 parquet shard，618 GiB），流式读取并打包，采用 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash 分词器 \cite{deepseek2024tokenizer}（词表 129,281）。
Complementary corpora---code (\texttt{UltraData-Code}), mathematics (\texttt{UltraData-Math}), curated web (\texttt{Ultra-FineWeb} tiers), and instruction-tuning sets (\texttt{UltraData-SFT-2605}, \texttt{UltraData-SFT-Agent-2609})---supply the production blend and the two decay segments.
% 中文：补充语料——代码（\texttt{UltraData-Code}）、数学（\texttt{UltraData-Math}）、精选网页（\texttt{Ultra-FineWeb} 各层）以及指令微调集（\texttt{UltraData-SFT-2605}、\texttt{UltraData-SFT-Agent-2609}）——为生产混合与两个衰减子段提供数据。
The stable phase draws on the seven-source mixture of Table~\ref{tab:stable-blend}, which keeps the $88{:}8{:}4$ web/code/math prior selected in Section~\ref{sec:mix-search} at the top level while promoting high-quality tiers (L1/L2) into the stable phase and reserving the L3 tiers for annealing.
% 中文：稳定阶段使用表 \ref{tab:stable-blend} 的七源混合：顶层保持第 \ref{sec:mix-search} 节选出的 $88{:}8{:}4$ 网页/代码/数学先验，同时把高质量层（L1/L2）提到稳定阶段，把 L3 层留给退火。
Sources are pre-blended into large shards before the run, so that each rank opens $\mathcal{O}(10)$ index files rather than the $\mathcal{O}(600)$ of the raw layout; this keeps the data-loader resident set bounded and is a precondition for the throughput reported in Section~\ref{sec:full-train}.
% 中文：各源在训练前被预混合为大 shard，使每个 rank 只需打开 $\mathcal{O}(10)$ 个索引文件，而非原始布局的 $\mathcal{O}(600)$；这使数据加载的常驻内存有界，也是第 \ref{sec:full-train} 节所报吞吐的前提。

Contamination control is enforced before any corpus enters training: a blacklist of $193{,}295$ character-level 13-gram fingerprints (FNV-1a 64-bit hashed, NFKC-normalised) is built from all $536$ tasks across six evaluation snapshots of EDA-Eval-PyAether, and every new data shard---including SFT corpora---is scanned for ${\geq}0.80$ n-gram overlap before release (Section~\ref{sec:posttrain-eval}).
% 中文：任何语料进入训练前都强制做污染控制：由 EDA-Eval-PyAether 六个评测快照的 $536$ 个任务构建 $193{,}295$ 条字符级 13-gram 指纹黑名单（FNV-1a 64 位哈希、NFKC 归一化），每个新数据 shard——包括 SFT 语料——在发布前都扫描 ${\geq}0.80$ 的 n-gram 重叠（第 \ref{sec:posttrain-eval} 节）。

\subsection{Long-Context Data Prerequisites}\label{sec:longctx-data}
Segment~B learns from whatever length structure the corpus actually contains, so before budgeting it we measured the document-length distribution of the main source with an EOD-aware scan ($\texttt{tokenizer\_eod}=129{,}280$; $8$ shards, $8.59$B tokens, $9.66$M documents).
% 中文：B 段学到的是语料实际包含的长度结构，因此在为其编列预算之前，我们用 EOD 感知扫描测量了主源的文档长度分布（$\texttt{tokenizer\_eod}=129{,}280$；$8$ 个 shard、$8.59$B token、$9.66$M 文档）。
The result is the binding constraint on Section~\ref{sec:longctx-train}: only $\mathbf{2.52\%}$ of tokens sit in documents of ${\geq}32$K, only $0.03\%$ reach ${\geq}128$K, and the mean document is $889$ tokens (Table~\ref{tab:longctx-data}).
% 中文：该结果是第 \ref{sec:longctx-train} 节的硬约束：仅 $\mathbf{2.52\%}$ 的 token 位于 ${\geq}32$K 的文档中，仅 $0.03\%$ 达到 ${\geq}128$K，平均文档长度 $889$ token（表 \ref{tab:longctx-data}）。
Training a 128K rung on this distribution would teach \emph{document concatenation} rather than long-context retrieval: the model would see 128K windows whose content is a chain of unrelated short documents, and its long-context score would be inflated relative to any real long document.
% 中文：在这样的分布上训练 128K 阶梯，教会的将是 \emph{文档拼接} 而非长上下文检索：模型看到的 128K 窗口内容是一串互不相关的短文档，其长上下文得分相对于任何真实长文档都会虚高。

We therefore treat the following as \emph{entry conditions} for segment~B, not as tuning knobs.
% 中文：因此我们把下列各项视为 B 段的 \emph{准入条件}，而非可调参数。
\textbf{(a) Long-document supplementation.}
Books, scientific articles, legal and standards text, and repository-level code (multi-file, in repository-consistent order) are added until the ${\geq}32$K token share reaches ${\geq}20\%$ and the ${\geq}128$K share reaches ${\geq}5\%$; until then the ladder stops at 64K.
% 中文：长度加权采样也同时采用，但它无法替代供给：对现有语料重新加权只能通过重复少量文档来提高 ${\geq}32$K 占比。
Length-weighted sampling is applied as well, but it cannot substitute for supply: re-weighting the present corpus raises the ${\geq}32$K share only by repeating a small number of documents.
% 中文：长度加权采样也同时采用，但它无法替代供给：对现有语料重新加权只能通过重复少量文档来提高 ${\geq}32$K 占比。
\textbf{(b) Document-level reset.}
Packed shards concatenate documents back-to-back, so segment~B requires the attention and Mamba-2 recurrent state to be reset at every EOD; without it a 128K window mixes documents and the position statistics the retrieval behaviour depends on are wrong.
% 中文：打包后的 shard 把文档首尾相接，因此 B 段要求在每处 EOD 重置注意力与 Mamba-2 循环状态；否则 128K 窗口会混合多篇文档，而检索行为所依赖的位置统计就错了。
\textbf{(c) Long-document de-duplication.}
Near-duplicate long documents are removed first, because a 128K rung consumes few optimisation steps relative to its token budget and duplication is amplified by length.
% 中文：近重复的长文档先行去除，因为 128K 阶梯相对其 token 预算只消耗很少优化步，重复会被长度放大。
Item~(b) is a framework capability rather than a data transformation; we list it here because it gates the same decision.
% 中文：第 (b) 项是框架能力而非数据变换；之所以在此列出，是因为它与本决策共用同一道门槛。

\subsection{Data Mixture Proportion Search}\label{sec:mix-search}
Because the optimal web/code/math ratio is scale-dependent and a full-scale sweep is infeasible, we searched the three-way mixture at a proxy scale using Bayesian optimisation.
% 中文：由于最优的网页/代码/数学比例依赖于规模，而全规模扫描不可行，我们以贝叶斯优化在代理规模上搜索了三路混合。
A compact proxy model ($d{=}128$, $L{=}14$, $\approx$18.36\,M parameters---two orders of magnitude smaller than the 2.2\,B target and not intended as a performance predictor) was trained for each trial under Optuna's \texttt{TPESampler} with \texttt{MedianPruner}, one trial per GPU, global batch size $2048$ at sequence length $4096$.
% 中文：一个紧凑代理模型（$d{=}128$、$L{=}14$、约 $18.36$M 参数——比最终骨干小两个数量级）在代理语料上训练，使每次试验的成本足够低，可支撑数百次试验。
The objective was the average accuracy on eight common-sense \texttt{lm-eval} tasks (\texttt{arc\_challenge}, \texttt{arc\_easy}, \texttt{boolq}, \texttt{hellaswag}, \texttt{openbookqa}, \texttt{piqa}, \texttt{sciq}, \texttt{winogrande}), each evaluated with \texttt{-{}-limit~500} subsampling.
% 中文：目标函数是八个常识类 \texttt{lm-eval} 任务的平均准确率（\texttt{arc\_challenge}、\texttt{arc\_easy} 等），在留出划分上测量。

Two rounds of 200 trials each were completed.
% 中文：共完成两轮、每轮 200 次试验。
In the first round, using final training loss as the objective, the Spearman rank correlation between loss-ranked and accuracy-ranked mixtures was $\rho{=}{-}0.43$, confirming that loss is a weak proxy for downstream ability.
% 中文：第一轮以最终训练损失为目标时，损失排序与八任务准确率排序之间的 Spearman 秩相关为负，提示损失在代理规模上不是下游能力的代理。
In the second round, using the eight-task average directly, the top-5 mixtures were retrained and re-evaluated at the full \texttt{lm-eval} scale.
% 中文：第二轮直接以八任务平均为目标，把前 5 个混合重新训练并在全规模上复评，以测试代理排序是否迁移。
Table~\ref{tab:mix-topk} reports the results: the full-eval rankings were nearly inverted relative to the Bayesian-optimisation rankings (Spearman $\rho{=}{-}0.80$, $n{=}5$, $p{\approx}0.10$), and the spread across all five mixtures was only $0.383$--$0.390$ ($\approx 0.6$\,pp).
% 中文：表 \ref{tab:mix-topk} 报告了结果：全量复评的排序相对贝叶斯排序几乎反转，即在此规模下该代理无法区分配比优劣。

\subsection{Long-Context Training: The Decay Tail of the WSD Schedule}\label{sec:longctx-train}

\noindent\textbf{Positioning: a segment of the decay phase, not a separate stage.}
Long-context training is executed as segment~B of the decay phase (Section~\ref{sec:wsd}), between the stable phase and post-training. It is not a standalone training stage, and it is placed neither in the stable phase nor in SFT/RL. Three properties of the schedule justify the placement.
% 中文：长上下文训练作为衰减阶段的 B 段执行（第 \ref{sec:wsd} 节），位于稳定阶段与后训练之间。它不是独立的训练阶段，既不放在稳定阶段，也不放在 SFT/RL 中。调度的三项性质支持这一定位。
\emph{(i) The learning rate is already low.} Segment~B runs under the tail of the decayed learning rate ($5.6\times10^{-4}$ at $p{=}0.25$ falling to $1.8\times10^{-4}$ at $p{=}0.75$; Table~\ref{tab:wsd}), and a distribution shift applied at small step sizes perturbs general capability least; applied at the peak learning rate, the same shift would overwrite the short-context features that the stable phase spent $2.94$T tokens acquiring.
\emph{(ii) SFT and RL do not establish ground retrieval ability.} SFT shapes instruction-following behaviour and RL optimises a policy preference over sampled outputs; neither exposes the model to the length-and-position distribution that retrieval depends on, and neither can be run at the token scale of segment~B.
\emph{(iii) The extension must precede post-training.} Post-training assumes a backbone that can already address a long window; if the 1M context of the product target were only an extrapolation of a backbone trained to 16K, every post-training run would inherit that limitation.

\noindent\textbf{Composition inside the long-context segment.}
Synthetic needle-in-a-haystack data occupies $20$--$40\%$ of the long-context tokens; natural long documents occupy the remainder. A purely synthetic segment is ruled out, because a model trained only on inserted needles learns to answer questions about inserted needles rather than to read long documents.
% 中文：合成大海捞针（needle-in-a-haystack）数据占长上下文 token 的 $20$–$40\%$；自然长文档占其余部分。纯合成长上下文被排除，因为只在插入的针上训练的模型会学会回答关于插入之针的问题，而不是学会阅读长文档。
The synthetic share is diversified over five protocols rather than one: single-needle NIAH; multi-needle MK-NIAH with distractor needles, where every needle must be returned for credit; multi-hop variable tracing; variable-length needles spanning one sentence to three paragraphs; and paraphrase-plus-noise variants in which the needle is reworded and the haystack is filled with topically related distractors.
% 中文：合成部分的协议被多样化为五种而非一种：单针 NIAH；带干扰针的多针 MK-NIAH（必须返回全部针才计分）；多跳变量追踪；跨一句至三段不等的变长针；以及改写加噪声变体——针被同义改写、haystack 填入主题相关的干扰段。
The natural share is drawn from the supplemented long-document sources of Section~\ref{sec:longctx-data} at building proportions of roughly $40\%$ books and scientific articles, $20\%$ repository-level code, and the remainder standards, legal, and documentation text; the haystack filler of the synthetic share is drawn from the same sources so that the two shares do not differ in style.
% 中文：自然部分按大致 $40\%$ 书籍与科技论文、$20\%$ 仓库级代码、其余为标准、法律与文档文本的构造比例，取自第 \ref{sec:longctx-data} 节补充后的长文档源；合成部分的 haystack 填充文本取自同一批源，使两部分在文风上不产生差异。
Insertion depth is sampled uniformly over $0$--$100\%$ instead of being concentrated at the middle, because the position sensitivity of a hybrid with four attention layers and $52$ recurrent layers is not uniform along the window.
% 中文：插入深度在 $0$–$100\%$ 上均匀采样，而非集中在中部，因为具备 4 个注意力层与 $52$ 个循环层的混合模型沿窗口的位置敏感性并不均匀。
\noindent\textbf{Training configuration.}
The ladder runs \mbox{32K$\to$64K$\to$128K} with context parallelism $\texttt{CP}\in\{2,4,8\}$ and $\texttt{DP}\in\{8,4,2\}$, holding $\texttt{seq}/\texttt{CP}=16{,}384$ tokens per GPU at every rung, and global batch size is held constant within a rung (Table~\ref{tab:longctx-train}).
% 中文：阶梯按 32K→64K→128K 运行，上下文并行 $\texttt{CP}\in\{2,4,8\}$、$\texttt{DP}\in\{8,4,2\}$，每一级都保持每 GPU $\texttt{seq}/\texttt{CP}=16{,}384$ token，且全局批大小在同一级内保持不变（表 \ref{tab:longctx-train}）。
The learning rate continues the decay and is never re-warmed: re-warming on entering a longer context would reintroduce the overwriting risk that motivated the placement in the first place.
% 中文：学习率延续衰减，绝不重新预热：在进入更长上下文时重新预热，会重新引入当初促使我们如此定位的那种覆盖风险。
Position encoding uses adjusted base frequency from the first training step---$\theta{=}10^{6}$ is fixed at initialisation, so the decay tail carries no post-hoc frequency change, and 128K is reached by training rather than only by extrapolation, leaving $1$M as an $8\times$ extrapolation.
% 中文：位置编码自第一步训练起即使用调整后的基底频率——$\theta{=}10^{6}$ 在初始化时即固定，因此衰减尾部不含任何事后频率变更；128K 由训练达成而非仅靠外推，1M 则保留为 $8\times$ 外推。
No position interpolation, NTK rescaling, or YaRN is enabled at any rung; a maximal-position change without the base-frequency change is excluded outright, because in our earlier sweep it produced NaN at every length tested.
% 中文：任何一级都不启用位置插值、NTK 重标定或 YaRN；只改最大位置而不改基底频率的做法被明确排除，因为在我们早前的扫描中它在所有长度上都产生 NaN。
Document-level reset is mandatory: attention and the Mamba-2 recurrent state are reset at each EOD, so a window contains documents rather than document fragments.
% 中文：文档级重置是强制的：注意力与 Mamba-2 循环状态在每处 EOD 重置，使窗口包含的是文档而非文档片段。

% 中文：当 WSD 调度结束时——即在两个衰减子段都完成之后——我们取一个快照并在其上测量 \emph{基础} 能力，与后训练结果分开报告（表 \ref{tab:longctx-eval}）：常识推理（CS8 与 CX）、针检索（passkey 与 RULER，1M 目标为 ${\geq}95\%$）、困惑度外推（$1$M$/4$K ${\leq}1.10\times$）以及基础格式遵循。

\noindent\textbf{Cost.}
At the measured 32K throughput and the extrapolated 64K/128K throughputs, the reference rung-cycle ($42$/$32$/$32$B) costs ${\approx}118$ GPU-hours on sixteen GPUs, of which the 32K rung alone is $33$ hours; the upper bound of Table~\ref{tab:longctx-budget} ($15\%$/40\%) costs ${\approx}220$ GPU-hours.
% 中文：按实测的 32K 吞吐与推断的 64K/128K 吞吐，参考轮次（$42$/$32$/$32$B）在十六张 GPU 上耗费约 $118$ GPU·h，其中仅 32K 一级就占 $33$ 小时；表 \ref{tab:longctx-budget} 的上界（$15\%$/40\%）耗费约 $220$ GPU·h。
The cost is carried by context parallelism rather than by additional nodes: 32K runs at $59\%$ of the 16K throughput, and that gap is the price of the training signal, not of the hardware.
% 中文：该成本由上下文并行承担，而非靠增加节点：32K 的运行速度为 16K 的 $59\%$，这一差距是训练信号的代价，而不是硬件的代价。

\noindent\textbf{Why the budget is this small.}
The long-context budget is deliberately an order of magnitude below the stable-phase budget, and the public precedents bound it from both sides.
% 中文：长上下文预算被刻意设定为比稳定阶段预算低一个数量级，而公开先例从两侧给出了界限。
ProLong-8B reaches a 128K context with state-of-the-art performance at its size after $40$B tokens of long-context training, which is already only $5\%$ of the long-context tokens used by Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct~\cite{gao2024prolong}; LongRoPE2 extends LLaMA3-8B to a 128K effective context with $10$B tokens while retaining $98.5\%$ of short-context performance~\cite{shang2025longrope2}.
% 中文：ProLong-8B 在 $40$B token 的长上下文训练后即达到 128K 上下文、并在同规模模型中取得最优表现，而这已仅是 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 所用长上下文 token 的 $5\%$ \cite{gao2024prolong}；LongRoPE2 用 $10$B token 把 LLaMA3-8B 扩展到 128K 有效上下文，同时保留 $98.5\%$ 的短上下文性能 \cite{shang2025longrope2}。
Our $88$--$176$B long-context budget (Table~\ref{tab:longctx-budget}) therefore sits above the public operating range, which is the conservative direction for a plan whose data gate (Section~\ref{sec:longctx-data}) is not yet satisfied.
% 中文：因此我们的 $88$–$176$B 长上下文预算（表 \ref{tab:longctx-budget}）位于公开运行区间之上，这对一份数据闸门（第 \ref{sec:longctx-data} 节）尚未满足的计划来说，是保守的方向。
Two qualifications keep the comparison honest: both precedents extend an \emph{already long-trained} base model by rescaling position encoding, whereas segment~B trains the length structure itself under a decayed learning rate; and neither operates at the $2.22$B scale, where the token requirement is not expected to exceed the $8$B-class figures above but is not measured here either.
% 中文：两点说明使该比较保持诚实：两个先例都是通过对位置编码做重标定来扩展一个 \emph{已受长训练} 的基础模型，而 B 段是在衰减学习率下训练长度结构本身；并且两者都不在 $2.22$B 规模上运行，在该规模下 token 需求预期不会超过上述 8B 级数字，但本文也未对此做测量。
Long-context ability is accordingly not bought with volume---it is bought with the right volume at the right point in the schedule---but the reference budget in Table~\ref{tab:longctx-budget} should be read as a floor that the data gate may push upward, not as a ceiling (estimate; no scale ablation of this axis is run in this paper).
% 中文：因此长上下文能力不是靠量堆出来的——而是靠在调度中正确的位置上投入正确的量——但表 \ref{tab:longctx-budget} 的参考预算应被理解为一道可能被数据闸门推高的 \emph{下限}，而非上限（估算；本文未对该轴做规模消融）。

\subsection{Long-Context Capability and Inference Cost}\label{sec:longctx}

The hybrid backbone's long-context behaviour was characterised along two axes: perplexity extrapolation and inference throughput under a production serving stack.
% 中文：混合骨干的长上下文行为沿两个维度加以刻画：困惑度外推，以及生产服务栈下的推理吞吐。

\textbf{Perplexity extrapolation.}
With adjusted base frequency (ABF, $\theta_{\text{rope}}{=}10^{6}$) applied to the four attention layers, the 20K-step ($\approx$655M-token) checkpoint sustains near-constant perplexity from 4K to 1M tokens without further training: PPL ranges from 51.5 (64K) to 55.4 (1M), a change of only $+7.6\%$ over a $256{\times}$ context increase (Table~\ref{tab:longctx-ppl}).
% 中文：对 4 个注意力层施加调整后的基底频率（ABF，$\theta_{\text{rope}}{=}10^{6}$）后，困惑度对直到 1M 的每一个长度都保持稳定。
This stability arises because only 4 of 56 layers carry RoPE position encoding; the 52 Mamba-2 state-space layers have no position dependency and therefore incur no out-of-distribution penalty at long range.
% 中文：这种稳定性源于仅 56 层中的 4 层携带 RoPE 位置编码；其余 52 个 Mamba-2 状态空间层与位置无关，因此外推负担只由这些层承担。

\textbf{Production training throughput.}
Two measurements fix the production configuration.
% 中文：两项测量确定了生产配置。
On a single $8{\times}$H100 node, the hybrid sustains $249\,040$ tok/s at $\texttt{TP}{=}1$/$\texttt{DP}{=}8$, micro-batch $2$, sequence $4096$ in BF16 (${\approx}600$ TFLOP/s/GPU).
% 中文：在单个 $8{\times}$H100 节点上，混合模型在 $\texttt{TP}{=}1$/$\texttt{DP}{=}8$、micro-batch $2$、序列 $4096$、BF16 下持续 $249\,040$ tok/s（约 $600$ TFLOP/s/GPU）。
At the production length the picture changes: with sequence $16{,}384$ and $\texttt{CP}{=}1$/$\texttt{DP}{=}16$ across two nodes, FP8 tensorwise with delayed scaling sustains $613{,}992$ tok/s, and the sustained rate observed on the live run is ${\approx}590$K tok/s over $7.08$--$7.32$ s per $4.19$M-token step.
% 中文：在生产长度下情形发生变化：序列 $16{,}384$、跨两节点 $\texttt{CP}{=}1$/$\texttt{DP}{=}16$ 时，FP8 tensorwise 延迟缩放持续 $613{,}992$ tok/s，而实际运行中观察到的持续速率约为 $590$K tok/s（每 $4.19$M token 步耗时 $7.08$–$7.32$ 秒）。
Computing-graph replay is disabled throughout: a replayed graph over a data-dependent Mamba-2 state ran into a throughput plateau, and disabling replay is a precondition for the rate above.
% 中文：全程关闭计算图重放：对带数据相关 Mamba-2 状态的计算图做重放会撞上吞吐平台期，关闭重放是达到上述速率的前提。

\textbf{Precision choice.}
At the $4096$-token screen the FP8 tile ($M{=}8192$) sits below the crossover point ($M^*{\approx}30$--$32$K) and ran at $0.91\times$ the BF16 throughput, so the screen retained BF16; a 1000-step A/B at $M{=}65{,}536$ additionally showed no persistent loss divergence between BF16 and delayed FP8 ($0.19\%$ end-of-run difference, one transient spike at step 700 recovering within ${\sim}60$ steps).
% 中文：在 $4096$ token 的筛选实验中，FP8 的 tile（$M{=}8192$）低于交叉点（$M^*{\approx}30$–$32$K），吞吐仅为 BF16 的 $0.91\times$，因此筛选阶段保留 BF16；而在 $M{=}65{,}536$ 上运行的 1000 步 A/B 进一步表明 BF16 与延迟 FP8 之间没有持续的损失分歧（末点差异 $0.19\%$，第 700 步有一次瞬态尖峰并在约 $60$ 步内恢复）。
At $M{=}16{,}384$ the tile is large enough for FP8 to pay, and the production run adopts FP8 tensorwise: the $613{,}992$ tok/s figure is an FP8 measurement, and the BF16 rate at the same shape is not directly measured.
% 中文：在 $M{=}16{,}384$ 时 tile 已足够大，使 FP8 划算，因此生产运行采用 FP8 tensorwise：$613{,}992$ tok/s 这一数字本身即 FP8 的测量值，而同一形状下的 BF16 速率未被直接测量。
The two results are therefore consistent rather than contradictory---BF16 is the right choice at short sequence length, FP8 at production length---and the decision was made per configuration, not globally.
% 中文：因此两项结果是一致的而非矛盾的——BF16 适合短序列长度，FP8 适合生产长度——该决策是按配置逐项作出的，而非全局统一。
