\section{Vision Encoder Pretraining}\label{sec:vision}

Stage~(iii) pre-trains a custom vision encoder from scratch for the multimodal stage (Section~\ref{sec:mllm}).
This section presents the architecture-selection rationale, the pre-training recipe, and the resulting measurements.

\subsection{Architecture Selection}\label{sec:vis-rationale}
We compare four candidate vision encoders at an iso-parameter $500$--$600$\,M scale, all \emph{randomly initialized} (no upstream ImageNet/CLIP/SigLIP weights) so that only the architecture body is contrasted.
All candidates are trained under a unified stack: \texttt{open\_clip}~3.2.0, a frozen shared text tower (12 layers, width~512, 8 heads, one BPE for all towers), the SigLIP sigmoid-contrastive objective, and a unified 500K subset of LLaVA-OneVision-1.5 (\texttt{imagenet/EN}), at resolution~224 / patch~16 / bf16, learning rate $10^{-3}$ (AdamW, warmup~100 + cosine), seed~1234, batch $32{\times}6$.
The four candidates are:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{OpenVision2} — a pure-attention ViT (width~1024, depth~30, heads~16, MLP~4096), $505.0$\,M parameters.
\item \textbf{MambaEye} — a pure visual state-space model (Vim-style bidirectional SSM, width~1024, depth~40, $d_{\mathrm{state}}{=}16$, expand~2), $534.9$\,M.
\item \textbf{MoE-ViE} — a sparse Mixture-of-Experts encoder (8 experts $\times$ ff~512, top-2 routing), $505.2$\,M total / $222.0$\,M active per token.
\item \textbf{DeepEncoderV2} — an Attention+SSM hybrid (Alternating Attention Mamba blocks), $516.9$\,M.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Finding.} Over $5000$--$10000$-step horizons all four architectures converge to $\approx4.45$--$4.47$ loss, with inter-architecture differences $<0.02$ (within multi-seed variance).
Loss is therefore \emph{architecture-invariant} at this scale and token count: at $224/16$ the encoder produces only $196$ tokens, too short for the SSM recurrence or MoE routing to amortize their per-token overhead into a quality gain.
Consequently the architecture choice is driven solely by efficiency, where the pure-attention \textbf{OpenVision2} dominates---$1.47\times$ the training throughput of the next-best (DeepEncoderV2) and $2.7\times$ lower inference latency, and full-SSM MambaEye even stalls at batch~1.

\textit{Methodological note.} The apparent ``loss plateau'' of $6.7342$ observed in a $1000$-step sweep is an artifact of the cosine schedule collapsing the learning rate to $\approx10^{-5}$ by $\sim$step~700; extending the cosine span to $5000$--$10000$ steps lets the loss continue to $\approx4.45$. Sub-1k-step comparisons of these encoders are therefore uninformative.
