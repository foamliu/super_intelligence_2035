\section{Introduction}

Large language models have recently demonstrated agentic capabilities---planning, tool invocation, and long-horizon reasoning---that make them attractive cores for intelligent assistants spanning chip design, verification, and other domains of electronic design automation.
% 中文：大语言模型近来展现出智能体能力——规划、工具调用与长程推理——这使其成为覆盖芯片设计、验证及其他电子设计自动化领域的智能助手的理想内核。
Such assistants are no longer hypothetical: agentic EDA flows are already deployed in-house, with Flash-tier masters such as DeepSeek-V4-Flash and GLM-5.3-Flash coordinating the flow.
% 中文：此类助手已非假设：智能体 EDA 流程已在公司内部署，由 DeepSeek-V4-Flash 与 GLM-5.3-Flash 等 Flash 档 master 负责协调。
The binding constraint is therefore cost rather than feasibility, and the cost structure is dominated by two terms: every EDA subtask routed to a master consumes tokens and serving budget that scale with design-flow volume, and because an agentic session accumulates context, what dominates serving cost is not prompt throughput but the memory that grows with the session.
% 中文：因此当前约束是成本而非可行性，而成本结构由两项主导：每个路由到 master 的 EDA 子任务都消耗随设计流程规模增长的 token 与 serving 预算；且由于智能体会话会累积上下文，主导服务成本的并非提示词吞吐，而是随会话增长的内存占用。
A small, locally served subagent cannot change either term at the master, but it caps the cost of every subtask it takes over by pricing it at a local 2.2B model rather than at a Flash master; and for a hybrid backbone the session-memory term is architectural as well, since only the four attention layers carry a session-growing state while the Mamba-2 layers hold a constant-size recurrent one.
% 中文：本地部署的小型子智能体无法改变 master 侧的这两项，但它把所接管的每个子任务按本地 22 亿参数模型而非 Flash master 计价，从而为成本设上界；而对混合骨干而言，会话内存项同样是架构决定的：只有 4 个注意力层携带随会话增长的状态，Mamba-2 层则为恒定大小的循环状态。

Recent small language models (SLMs, $<\,$2\,B) have closed much of the gap with larger counterparts on single-turn benchmarks, but building such a model \emph{from scratch} as a production subagent remains an under-documented engineering problem.
% 中文：近期的小语言模型（SLM，$<$ 20 亿参数）已在单轮基准上大幅缩小与更大模型的差距，但从零构建这样一个模型并将其用作生产级子智能体，仍是一个缺乏文档记录的工程问题。

\paragraph{Contributions.}
We present BaiZe, a $\approx$2.2B-parameter hybrid (state-space + attention) agentic model built from scratch to serve as a cost-efficient EDA subagent beneath a deployed Flash master.
% 中文：我们提出 BaiZe——一个约 22 亿参数的混合（状态空间+注意力）智能体模型，从零构建，作为已部署 Flash master 之下具备成本效益的 EDA 子智能体。

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A cost-efficient EDA subagent.}
BaiZe receives EDA subtasks in natural language from the master and drives EDA tools in their native scripting languages (PyAether, SKILL, Tcl).
% 中文：高性价比的 EDA 子智能体。BaiZe 以自然语言从 master 接收 EDA 子任务，并以 EDA 工具的原生脚本语言（PyAether、SKILL、Tcl）驱动这些工具。
Its 56 layers interleave 52 Mamba-2 state-space layers with four grouped-query-attention layers, so only the latter carry a session-growing state; linear-time decoding whose serving cost is governed by session memory, rather than by prompt throughput, is what makes per-subtask cost reduction structurally plausible rather than a matter of quantization alone.
% 中文：其 56 层将 52 个 Mamba-2 状态空间层与 4 个分组查询注意力层交错排列，因此只有后者携带随会话增长的状态；解码为线性时间，其服务成本由会话内存而非提示词吞吐决定——这使每子任务成本下降在结构上可行，而非仅靠量化。
On this backbone, a staged budget-constrained search (S1--S5) with a reduced-horizon proxy protocol fixes the pre-training recipe.
% 中文：在此骨干之上，分阶段、受预算约束的搜索（S1--S5）配合缩短视野的代理协议确定预训练配方。
We then evaluate BaiZe on a 449-task EDA-scripting benchmark spanning three languages and four commercial runtimes, asking two questions: whether it matches the deployed Flash masters at comparable task accuracy while materially reducing per-subtask serving cost, and what an on-premise subagent must co-locate with the model---whether a sandbox and multi-round refinement justify their serving cost, or whether a lighter single-shot configuration suffices.
% 中文：随后我们在一个覆盖三种语言、四种商业 runtime 的 449 任务 EDA 脚本基准上评估 BaiZe，提出两个问题：它能否以可比的任务精度匹配已部署的 Flash master 同时显著降低每子任务服务成本；以及内网子智能体必须与模型共同部署什么——沙箱与多轮精炼是否值得其服务成本，还是更轻的单发配置即足够。

\item \textbf{Multimodal grounding for visual EDA artifacts.}
Layout, schematic, and waveform views carry much of the correctness signal in custom and analog design, yet a text-only subagent cannot observe them directly.
% 中文：面向视觉 EDA 产物的多模态 grounding。版图、原理图与波形视图承载了定制与模拟设计中大量的正确性信号，然而纯文本子智能体无法直接观测它们。
BaiZe pairs its from-scratch language core with a from-scratch vision encoder and tests whether its 196-token-per-image budget suffices for such detail-dense views, or whether finer token budgets are required for reliable visual grounding.
% 中文：BaiZe 将其从零训练的语言内核与从零训练的视觉编码器配对，检验每图 196 token 的预算是否足以应对这类细节密集的视图，还是需要更细的 token 预算才能实现可靠的 visual grounding。
Measured results: the encoder reaches $68.2\%$ linear-probe on an internal protocol, yet the MLLM stage averages $26.1\%$ over 11 benchmarks, indicating that encoder-quality gains do not transfer downstream at the $224$-px single-tile setting.
% 中文：实测结果：编码器在内部协议上线性探测达 68.2\%，但 MLLM 阶段在 11 个基准上仅平均 26.1\%，表明编码器质量的增益在 224 像素单块设定下未迁移到下游。
\end{itemize}

We test this architecture rather than assume it: the question we put to the benchmark is whether a small local subagent is viable at production scale, and where its cost--accuracy point lands.
% 中文：我们检验而非假定该架构：我们向基准提出的问题是，本地小型子智能体在生产规模上是否可行，以及它的成本—精度点落在何处。
