\section{Conclusion}

We presented BaiZe, a $\approx$2.2B-parameter hybrid (state-space + attention) agentic language model built from scratch on a fixed $\sim$84\,GPU-hour budget.
Through a controlled from-scratch comparison, we selected a Nemotron-H-style Mamba2-hybrid backbone over a dense transformer for its lower loss-per-parameter and substantially faster inference, then applied a staged, budget-constrained hyper-parameter search (S1--S4) on the final backbone.
Training and inference measurements are being re-executed on the final model and are reported as \textbf{[TBD]}; the complete recipe and a single reproducible command encapsulate the entire construction under the NVIDIA NeMo recipe.

The immediate next step is agentic fine-tuning with the \texttt{UltraData-SFT-Agent} corpus toward a deployable agent core for EDA and embedded applications.
We hope this blueprint lowers the barrier for cost-bounded teams building their own efficient agentic SLMs from scratch.
