\section{Per-task cost across ablation configurations}
\label{app:cost}

Table~\ref{tab:cost} reports per-task cost on the 291-task audited subset (SKILL and the two Tcl hosts; PyAether is pending audit), so these figures compare configurations on a fixed task set rather than against the pooled 449-task accuracy. Execution dominates cost, and retrieval pays for itself: with retrieval removed the agent falls back on blind sandbox probing, making it the most expensive configuration---2.3$\times$ the full system's solve time at nearly identical token counts. Self-exploration is the one cost-negative rung: enriched documentation cuts \texttt{search\_apis} calls by 11.0\%, \texttt{run\_code} by 12.7\%, and rounds by 10.1\%, reducing net-active solve time by 27.0\% and input tokens by 8.7\%, while \texttt{get\_api\_details} is essentially unchanged ($-3.3\%$)---better documentation means fewer exploratory queries, not more reading.

\section{Effect of bounding the evidence available to \texorpdfstring{$\Phi$}{Phi}}
\label{app:phi-bound}

Table~\ref{tab:phi-bound} isolates the belief-update budget $\Phi$ (LLM, $\Omega$, and effector fixed) on the PyAether slice. Accuracy rises monotonically with the execution budget $k$ (unsaturated at $k{=}10$); a \emph{lagged} channel that returns the previous execution's state lands between $k{=}3$ and $k{=}10$, confirming that stale evidence is worth markedly less than fresh (Principle~3); and tighter budgets leave traces unfinished (Converged drops from $99.1$ to $76.3$ at $k{=}1$). The SKILL/Tcl/Overall cells for the budget arms await the cross-slice runs.
