\section{Theoretical Grounding: The Grounding Gap}
\label{sec:grounding-theory}

Our empirical results show that the execution loop is decisive: adding sandbox execution to retrieval yields +9.2~pp ($p{<}10^{-8}$), while removing execution ($-9.2$~pp) or retrieval ($-0.4$~pp, n.s.) reduces accuracy. This section explains \emph{why} this is structural rather than implementation-specific: EDA scripting correctness is a \emph{situated} property that cannot be guaranteed from offline knowledge, and execution is the only channel that observes the current design state. The gap is bounded by observation fidelity, not model scale.

% 本节解释为什么这不是实现的偶然，而是EDA脚本编写的结构性属性。
% 我们浓缩"落地鸿沟"框架：EDA脚本正确性是一个情境化属性，无法从离线知识保证，执行是观测当前设计状态的唯一通道。
% 该鸿沟受观测保真度约束，而非模型规模。

\subsection{A Four-Level Hierarchy of Information}
\label{sec:four-levels}

To make precise what can be learned offline versus what must be observed online, we distinguish four levels of information:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{L1--Syntactic Correctness}: well-formedness of symbols; acquirable offline.
\item \textbf{L2--Physical Plausibility}: robust statistical patterns of how the world behaves; acquirable offline as the agent's \emph{prior}.
\item \textbf{L3--Historical Factuality}: what has occurred or is documented; past-oriented, acquirable offline, but with no connection to what is true \emph{now}.
\item \textbf{L4--Situated Truthfulness}: what is true \emph{right now} in this specific environment---\emph{indexical} (truth depends on context and changes over time), \emph{non-guaranteeable from offline data}, and requiring \emph{real-time commitment}. \textbf{Not learnable offline.}
\end{itemize}
L4 is not a semantic category but a \emph{grounding requirement} imposed by indexical reference; the four levels separate what can be \emph{known in advance} from what must be \emph{observed now}---L4 is read from the environment, not recalled from memory.

For EDA scripting, the situated state is the live design database: which cell and view are selected, what the layout contains at this moment. The correctness criterion---generated code executes without error and all assertions over the mutated design pass---is an L4 property: a script that is well-formed (L1), plausible (L2), and invokes documented functions (L3) can nonetheless violate an undocumented binding contract or depend on an internal selection state. Such failures must be \emph{observed} by acting on the live design and reading back the result.

% 我们采用的正确性判据——生成代码无错误执行且针对被修改设计的所有断言通过——是一个L4属性。
% 一个语法正确（L1）、合理（L2）、调用已文档化函数（L3）的脚本，仍可能违反未文档化的绑定契约、
% 依赖内部选中状态或定位到错误单元而失败。离线知识无法解决这类失败；它们只能通过作用于实时设计并读回结果来观测。

\subsection{A Bayesian Formalization of the Grounding Gap}
\label{sec:bayesian}

We cast grounding as decision-making under partial observability. Let $\Theta$ denote the space of L4 states, $o_t$ a runtime observation, $a_t$ a code action, and $\mathcal{K}$ the offline knowledge in the LLM's frozen parameters (L1--L3). The agent maintains a belief $b_t(\theta) \triangleq p(\theta_t \mid o_{1:t}, a_{1:t-1}, \mathcal{K})$, updated by Bayes' rule:
\begin{equation}
b_t(\theta) \propto \underbrace{p(o_t \mid \theta_t)}_{\Omega}\, \int_{\Theta} \underbrace{p(\theta_t \mid \theta_{t-1}, a_{t-1})}_{\mathcal{T}}\, b_{t-1}(\theta_{t-1})\, d\theta_{t-1},
\end{equation}
where $\Omega$ is the \emph{observation likelihood} (the Harness's observation modules) and $\mathcal{T}$ the \emph{transition model} (the LLM's prior over how EDA operations transform the design). Actions minimize expected cost $a_t^* = \arg\min_{a} \mathbb{E}_{\theta_t \sim b_t}[\mathcal{C}(\theta_t, a, G) + \gamma V(b_{t+1})]$.

% 令$\Theta$为L4状态空间（在线EDA设计数据库），$o_t$为运行时观测，$a_t$为生成的代码动作，
% $\mathcal{K}$为编码在LLM冻结参数中的离线知识（L1–L3）。智能体维护关于当前L4状态的信念$b_t(\theta)$，
% 通过贝叶斯法则递归更新。其中观测似然$\Omega$由框架的观测模块实现（第~\ref{sec:zhuLong-harness}节），
% $\mathcal{T}$为LLM先验提供的转移模型。行动通过最小化期望代价选择。

The \emph{grounding gap} is the divergence between the posterior with runtime observations and the posterior from the offline prior alone:
\begin{equation}
D_{\text{grounding}}(t) \triangleq D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(p(\cdot \mid o_{1:t},\, \mathcal{K},\, \text{env}) \;\middle\|\; p_{\mathcal{K}}(\cdot \mid o_{1:t})\right).
\end{equation}
This divergence is bounded by observation fidelity, not model capacity. If $I(\Theta_t; O_t \mid \mathcal{K}) = 0$---the observation channel is silent---no prior improvement can recover the current state. Scaling $\mathcal{K}$ improves L1--L3 but cannot push the gap below the floor imposed by $\Omega$. The source is causal: parameters are fixed before deployment, and the environment at time $t$ is not a function of the training corpus. This separates grounding from learning-based adaptation: fine-tuning improves the prior over L1--L3 during \emph{training}; grounding requires the likelihood and a posterior update from observing $o_t$ during \emph{deployment}.

% 这把落地与基于学习的适应区分开：微调与元学习是\emph{训练}期获得的能力，改善的是关于L1–L3分布的先验；
% 落地发生在\emph{部署}期，需要似然与基于观测$o_t$的后验更新。更好的先验并不能消除对观测的需要；
% 两者互补——一个离线、一个在线；一个关乎知识、一个关乎信念。

\subsection{ZhuLong as an Instance of the Agent Harness}
\label{sec:zhuLong-harness}

The formulation maps onto ZhuLong's architecture: the frozen LLM supplies $\mathcal{K}$ (L1--L3), $\mathcal{T}$, and $\Pi$; the Harness supplies observation, belief update, and execution. \texttt{search\_apis}/\texttt{get\_api\_details} are the observation modules $\Omega$; \texttt{run\_code} is the effector acting on the live design database; the re-planning loop implements $\Phi$. The closed loop $b_t \to a_t \to o_{t+1} \to b_{t+1}$ turns one-shot generation into grounded search.

\subsection{Design Principles}
\label{sec:prediction}

Three principles follow: \emph{freeze} $\mathcal{K}$, \emph{maximize} $\Omega$'s fidelity, and \emph{run} $\Phi$ \emph{continuously}. (1)~Decouple knowledge from belief: $\mathcal{K}$ is the static L1--L3 prior in the LLM; $b_t$ is the dynamic posterior maintained by the Harness. Freezing $\mathcal{K}$ and absorbing adaptation in $b_t$ yields stability and flexibility. (2)~Optimize observation over the prior: the gap is bounded by $\Omega$'s fidelity, not model scale; observation investment has direct bounded effect, while model scaling has diminishing returns once the prior is rich. (3)~Design for continuous belief updating: $\Phi$'s update rate must exceed L4 dynamics, and in an ICL-type autonomous agent the quantity to bound is how much evidence $b_t$ may accumulate---a graded execution budget or a lagged read-back.

These principles \emph{account for} our ablation findings: removing $\Omega$ (w/o Sandbox) makes L4 violations unobservable ($-9.2$~pp); RAG improves the L1--L3 prior but cannot observe L4 (ceiling 72.2\%). Three diagnostic questions follow for any candidate system: (1)~does it implement $\Omega$ with sufficient fidelity? (2)~does it maintain $b_t$ through $\Phi$? (3)~does it execute $a_t$ through a causal interface? These organize the evaluation into RQ1--RQ3 (Section~\ref{sec:exp-setup}).
