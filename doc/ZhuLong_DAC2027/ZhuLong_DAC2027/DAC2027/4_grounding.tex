\section{Theoretical Grounding: The Grounding Gap}
\label{sec:grounding-theory}

This section develops the \emph{grounding gap} framework, stated before the measurements of Section~\ref{sec:experiments} so that its claims are predictions the experiments could refute rather than explanations fitted afterwards: EDA correctness is a \emph{situated} property that cannot be guaranteed from offline knowledge, execution is the only channel that observes the current design state, and the resulting gap is bounded by observation fidelity, rather than model scale.
% 本节提出"落地鸿沟"框架，表述先于 §6 测量，使主张成为可反驳预测而非事后拟合：EDA 正确性是情境化属性，无法从离线知识保证；执行是观测当前设计状态的唯一通道；该鸿沟受观测保真度而非模型规模约束。

\subsection{A Four-Level Hierarchy of Information}
\label{sec:four-levels}

To separate what can be learned offline from what must be observed online, Table~\ref{tab:four-levels} distinguishes four levels: L1 \emph{syntactic correctness}, L2 \emph{physical plausibility}, and L3 \emph{historical factuality} (documented, past-oriented, frozen at the training cutoff) are learnable offline, whereas L4 \emph{situated truthfulness}---what is true \emph{right now} in a specific environment---is not, defined by indexicality (truth depends on context and changes over time), non-guaranteeability from offline data, and real-time commitment. L4 must be \emph{read} from the environment, not \emph{recalled} from memory.
% 四级信息层次（表~\ref{tab:four-levels}）：L1 语法正确性、L2 物理合理性、L3 历史事实性可离线获取；L4 情境真实性（此刻为真）不可，由指示性、无法由离线数据保证、实时承诺三特征定义。非知识分类法，而是"可预先知道"与"必须此刻观测"的分离：L4 是必须从环境读取而非从记忆回忆的东西。

\begin{table*}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{The four levels of information}
\label{tab:four-levels}
\begin{tabular}{clp{5.2cm}c}
\toprule
\textbf{Level} & \textbf{Name} & \textbf{Core Question} & \textbf{Learnable Offline?} \\
\midrule
L1 & Syntactic Correctness & Is this string well-formed? & Yes \\

% 表：四个信息层次。"能否离线学习"一列是操作性判据：L1–L3 可以，L4 不可以。

For EDA scripting, the situated state is the live design database---which cell/view is selected and what the layout or schematic contains now---plus the user's current intent (a user clarification is also an L4 observation). Our correctness criterion---code runs without error and all assertions over the mutated design pass---is L4: a well-formed (L1), plausible (L2) script invoking documented functions (L3) can still violate an undocumented binding contract, depend on internal selection state, or target the wrong cell. Such failures cannot be resolved offline; they must be \emph{observed} by acting on the live design and reading back the result.
% 对 EDA 脚本，情境状态是实时设计数据库（当前选中 cell/view、此刻版图/原理图内容）及用户当前意图（故用户澄清亦为 L4 观测）。正确性判据——代码无错执行且对被修改设计的所有断言通过——是 L4 属性：语法正确、合理、调用已文档化函数的脚本仍可能违反未文档化绑定契约、依赖内部选中状态或定位错误 cell。离线知识无法解决，必须作用于实时设计并读回结果来观测。

\subsection{A Bayesian Formalization of the Grounding Gap}
\label{sec:bayesian}

We cast grounding as decision-making under partial observability \cite{kaelbling1998planning, thrun2005probabilistic}; the formulation is diagnostic rather than algorithmic---it makes precise what is missing (the observation likelihood $\Omega$ and the recursive update $\Phi$) without prescribing tractable inference.
% 把落地建模为部分可观测下决策；形式化是诊断性而非算法性——精确指出缺失什么（观测似然 Ω 与递归更新 Φ）。

Let $\Theta$ denote the space of L4 states (the live EDA design database), $o_t$ a runtime observation, $a_t$ a generated code action, and $\mathcal{K}$ the offline knowledge (L1--L3) in the LLM's frozen parameters. The agent maintains a belief $b_t(\theta)\triangleq p(\theta_t\mid o_{1:t},\,a_{1:t-1},\,\mathcal{K})$ over the current L4 state, updated recursively by Bayes' rule:
\begin{equation}
b_t(\theta) \propto p(o_t \mid \theta_t)\, \int_{\Theta} p(\theta_t \mid \theta_{t-1}, a_{t-1})\, b_{t-1}(\theta_{t-1})\, d\theta_{t-1},
\end{equation}
where $p(o_t \mid \theta_t)$ is the \emph{observation likelihood} $\Omega$ and $p(\theta_t \mid \theta_{t-1}, a_{t-1})$ is the \emph{transition model} $\mathcal{T}$ supplied by the LLM's prior; actions are chosen by an approximate policy $\Pi$ that minimizes expected cost under $b_t$, with $\mathcal{C}$ encoding the task assertions. Eq.~(1) is the standard POMDP filter \cite{kaelbling1998planning}; what is new here is not the filter but the \emph{implementation} of $\Omega$ as retrieval plus execution read-back, which is this work's contribution to the POMDP stack.
% 令 Θ 为 L4 状态空间，o_t 观测，a_t 代码动作，K 为 LLM 冻结参数中的离线知识。信念 b_t 经贝叶斯递归更新（Eq.1）。Ω=观测似然，T=LLM 先验给的转移模型，Π=最小化期望代价的近似策略，C=任务断言。Eq.1 是标准 POMDP 滤波；本文新意不在滤波器，而在把 Ω 实现为"检索+执行回读"——这是本工作对 POMDP 栈的贡献。

The \emph{grounding gap} is the divergence between the posterior achievable with runtime observations and the posterior from the offline prior alone:
\begin{equation}
D_{\text{grounding}}(t) \triangleq D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(p(\cdot \mid o_{1:t},\, \mathcal{K},\, \text{env}) \;\middle\|\; p_{\mathcal{K}}(\cdot)\right),
\end{equation}
where the right-hand side is the \emph{pure prior} unconditioned on any observation. We now bound this gap.
% 落地鸿沟 = 有运行时观测的后验与仅靠离线先验的后验之间的散度（Eq.2）。右边是\emph{纯先验}，不条件于任何观测（10:2x §③ 修正符号错误：去掉 |o_{1:t}）。下面给出该鸿沟的上界。

\textbf{Scenario: the transition is deterministic.} In EDA, \texttt{run\_code} executes a script whose effect on the design database is deterministic: the same action in the same state yields the same result, so $\mathcal{T}$ is exact---a property of the domain, not a simplifying assumption. The only mismatch is in the observation likelihood $\Omega$: retrieval may return incomplete documentation, and execution read-back may truncate---precisely what $\epsilon$ captures. Model mismatch under estimated parameters is a recognized obstacle \cite{delage2010percentile}; our setting is milder, with only $\Omega$, not $\mathcal{T}$, possibly mismatched.
% 场景论证（10:2x §②a + 15:4x 新增 Delage&Mannor 2010 支撑"模型失配是公认问题"）：EDA 中 run_code 执行脚本对设计库的改动是确定性的——同动作在同状态产生同结果，无动作随机性——故 T 精确。这是领域真实属性而非简化假设。唯一失配在观测似然 Ω：检索可能返回不完整文档、执行回读可能截断。这正是 ε 要刻画的。模型失配是参数估计下序贯决策的公认障碍 \cite{delage2010percentile}；我们的设定是更温和的情形：只有 Ω 可能失配，T 不会。

\textbf{Claim (observation-fidelity bound).} Let $\epsilon \triangleq \sup_{\theta}\, \| p(\cdot\mid\theta) - \hat{p}(\cdot\mid\theta) \|_{\mathrm{TV}}$, the total-variation distance between the true and the agent-used observation likelihoods. We posit that $D_{\text{grounding}} \le g(\epsilon)$, with $g(\epsilon)\to 0$ as $\epsilon\to 0$ and $g$ \emph{independent of the complexity of the prior} $\mathcal{K}$: the grounding gap is bounded by observation fidelity, rather than model scale. We state this as a \emph{prediction the experiments can test} rather than a theorem derived here; the next paragraph tests it from both sides.
% 命题（观测保真度上界，15:4x 修正：Lemma→Claim，去掉"由标准 POMDP 模型近似结果"归因，改为"我们提出"）：ε ≜ sup_θ ‖p(·|θ) − p̂(·|θ)‖_TV（真实与 agent 所用观测似然的全变差）。我们提出 D_grounding ≤ g(ε)，g(ε)→0 (ε→0)，g 不依赖先验 K 的复杂度：落地鸿沟受观测保真度而非模型规模约束。作为"实验可检验的预测"而非此处推导的定理提出，下一段从两侧检验。

\textbf{Experimental correspondence (developed in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).} Here $\epsilon$ is exactly the fidelity of this paper's $\Omega$ implementation---retrieval quality plus execution read-back. The read-back ladder (N$'$ $\to$ B $\to$ F, Table~\ref{tab:omega}) varies $\epsilon$ with retrieval and the effector fixed; the backbone axis (Table~\ref{tab:llm-comparison}) varies $\mathcal{K}$ with $\epsilon$ fixed. The experiments test whether the former axis dominates, as the claim predicts.
% 实验对应（10-26 修正：ladder 与 ε axis 分开表述，避免归因混淆）：回读阶梯 N′→B→F（表~\ref{tab:omega}）固定检索+执行器只改回读，单调增益 7.1pp（N′→F）反映单变量切片下 g(ε) 的单调性；系统级 ε 轴——纯 LLM 无观测到完整系统——跨 57.2pp。backbone 轴（表~\ref{tab:llm-comparison}）改 K 而 ε 固定，其 23.7pp 跨度小于系统级 ε 轴的 57.2pp——与"g 不依赖 K"一致。

\subsection{Design Principles}
\label{sec:prediction}

The formalization yields three architectural principles.
\textbf{Decouple knowledge from belief:} adapt in the dynamic posterior $b_t$ the Harness maintains, not by updating the frozen prior $\mathcal{K}$.
\textbf{Optimize observation over the prior:} the gap is bounded by $\Omega$'s fidelity ($g(\epsilon)$, independent of model scale), so observation investment pays directly while a richer prior gives diminishing returns.
\textbf{Design for continuous belief updating:} $\Phi$ runs online ($b_t\mapsto b_{t+1}$); in the ICL-type agent (Section~\ref{sec:system}) what is bounded is not a round count but how much evidence belief can accumulate---a graded execution budget or a lagging read-back.
% 三条架构原则（10:0x §④ / 报告 #9：压成标题+一句）。（一）解耦知识与信念：K=静态先验，b_t=动态后验，适应吸收在 b_t 而非部署期更新 K。（二）优先优化观测而非先验：鸿沟受 Ω 保真度约束（g(ε) 与模型规模无关），观测投入有直接有界效果，模型规模在先验足够丰富后收益递减。（三）为持续信念更新而设计：Φ 在线运行；§3 的 ICL 型智能体无轮数，需约束的是信念能积累多少证据——执行的分级预算，或滞后于其报告动作的回读。
