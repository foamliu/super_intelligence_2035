\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

ZhuLong reframes EDA scripting as the connective tissue that makes design flows runnable end-to-end: a general coding agent orchestrates flows and synthesizes the glue where closed tools leave gaps. The grounding-gap framework explains why: the gap on L4 failures is bounded by observation fidelity, while scale still sets the L1--L3 ceiling. The full system reaches \textbf{76.9\%} pooled Pass@1. Future work: domain-specializing the harness, long-term memory and recursive self-improvement, multimodal grounding, and long-chain flow-orchestration benchmarking---the glue layer ZhuLong evaluates is the precondition of a whole-flow agent.
