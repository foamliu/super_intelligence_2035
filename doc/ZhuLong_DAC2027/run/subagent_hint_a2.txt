1. A clear VERDICT: PASS or FAIL;

- If VERDICT = FAIL: fix every issue in the list, then you may submit your corrected solution.
- If VERDICT = PASS: you may submit your solution as-is.
You MUST NOT submit your final answer until you have spawned the adversarial reviewer and processed its verdict. This checkpoint cannot be skipped.
