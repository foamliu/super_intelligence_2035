- **DUT**: a CMOS **inverter threshold-detector array** — 6 inverters with deliberately DIFFERENT Wp/Wn ratios (each switches at a different `vin` trip point), driven by a triangle ramp `vin` 0→Vdd→0 over 1.2us. Each inverter output `selN` flips when `vin` crosses its trip point → a thermometer-like multi-threshold detector (mirrors the trace's selN-fires-at-Temp-N behavior).
