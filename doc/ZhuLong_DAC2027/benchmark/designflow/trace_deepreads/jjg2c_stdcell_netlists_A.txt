This segment connects the vertical wire from I554 AD area (Y=790)
to the vertical wire going to I564 QN / I599 OUT (Y=825).

The two diagonals are SYMMETRIC — they mirror each other, forming an X-like
crossing pattern between the middle row (I554 at Y=660) and top row (I559/I564 at Y=850).

In ViewDraw, S segments can connect any two junctions, including diagonal.
In Virtuoso layout, these should be routed as L-shaped (orthogonal) paths,
NOT as actual diagonal metal wires. The diagonal in the schematic is a
drawing convenience to show the connection crossing between rows.

How to detect diagonal wires in any schematic:
For each N block, collect J records (junctions with x,y).
For each S <a> <b> record, compute dx = J[b].x - J[a].x, dy = J[b].y - J[a].y.
If dx != 0 AND dy != 0: this segment is DIAGONAL (non-orthogonal).
Report all diagonal segments with their net name and coordinates.

################################################################################
# VIRTUOSO TIPS
################################################################################

1. Build cells bottom-up: NOT -> TRI_NOTAD -> LATCH05C / BUF2X6 -> D_KD145

2. Multi-finger NOT: 2x = mp=2, 6x = mp=6, per-finger W unchanged

3. VDD/GND: M1 horizontal strips at top/bottom of each cell

4. Pin placement in Virtuoso must match symbol P-record positions:
- NOT:       A on LEFT, QN on RIGHT
- TRI_NOTAD: AD on LEFT, QN on RIGHT, B on TOP, C on BOTTOM
- LATCH05C:  D on LEFT-upper, CK on LEFT-lower, Q on RIGHT-upper, Q~ on RIGHT-lower
- BUF2X6:    IN on LEFT, OUT on RIGHT (internal: two NOTs side by side horizontally, NOT stacked)
- NMOS/PMOS: G on LEFT, D on TOP, S on BOTTOM

5. Bus positions in D_KD145 (CRITICAL — buses are at different X positions):
- CK bus: FAR LEFT, to the LEFT of LATCH column. Connects rightward to LATCH LEFT-lower.
- OS bus: CENTER, BETWEEN LATCH column and TRI_NOTAD column.
Connects rightward to NOT inverter LEFT, and to TRI_NOTAD BOTTOM(C).
- CK bus and OS bus are NOT at the same horizontal position.
The LATCH column sits between them.
- QN merge line: at RIGHT side of TRI_NOTAD column, the rightmost vertical line before BUF2X6.

6. Wire routing by pin position (with crossing columns):
- CK bus -> LATCH LEFT-lower(CK): short horizontal, Column 1 to Column 2 (adjacent)
- LATCH RIGHT-lower(Q~) -> TRI_NOTAD LEFT(AD): long horizontal,
Column 2 -> crosses Column 3 (OS) -> Column 4 (passes between OS inverter cells)
- OS inverter RIGHT(QN) -> TRI_NOTAD TOP(B): L-shaped (right then up), Column 3 to Column 4
- OS bus -> TRI_NOTAD BOTTOM(C): vertical wire, from OS bus up to TRI_NOTAD bottom pin
- TRI_NOTAD RIGHT(QN) -> BUF2X6 LEFT(IN): horizontal, Column 4 to Column 5 (adjacent)
- LATCH Q (RIGHT-upper) = N/C: leave unconnected

7. In LATCH05C internal layout:
- I567 NOT at BOTTOM (clock inverter), I559 TRI_NOTAD + I564 NOT at TOP (side by side),
I554 TRI_NOTAD alone in MIDDLE. CK/CK~ use net-by-name (multiple net IDs, same label).
Positions verified from I-line X,Y coordinates.
- CK enters from LEFT-lower, goes UP to NOT I567.
- D enters from LEFT-upper, goes DOWN to I554 LEFT(AD).
- Q exits from I554 RIGHT, goes UP to RIGHT-upper pin.
- Q~ exits from I559 RIGHT, goes DOWN to RIGHT-lower pin AND to NOT I564.
- Q_fb from NOT I564 goes UP to I559 LEFT(AD) — U-shaped feedback path.
