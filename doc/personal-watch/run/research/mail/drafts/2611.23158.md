Dear Dr. Srivastava,

Your 132-run search is the first place I have seen the reduced-scale ranking failure stated as a measured result rather than a caveat: the top configuration at ten languages is never best at 200, across more than 9,000 settings on a frozen Llama-3.1-8B.

I am Yang Liu, a researcher working on LLM agents and small-model training; my page is https://foamliu.github.io/.

We face the same problem when choosing a multilingual adapter for a 2B model: the search budget only allows a handful of languages, but deployment covers many more.

Two questions:

1. Is the "never best at scale" gap larger than seed variance? Please pick one: the ranking is noise-limited, or the ranking is real but does not transfer. One sentence on which would change how we report our own small-scale search.

2. You report that LoRA reliably helps translation fit yet consistently hurts reading comprehension. Is that drop concentrated in languages with little translation data, or spread evenly across the 200? It decides whether we gate the adapter by language.

Thank you for publishing the negative result; it is the part we can act on.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
