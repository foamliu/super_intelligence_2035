## Subject

A follow-up on winning-ticket identification: iteration budget and warmup

## Body

Dear Professor Frankle,

I am Yang Liu, a researcher working on LLM agents; you can find my work at https://foamliu.github.io/.

I read The Lottery Ticket Hypothesis while studying why sparse subnetworks in our small-model training fail to reproduce dense accuracy. Your finding that winning tickets under 10-20% of the original size retrain to comparable accuracy, and above that size learn faster, is the baseline I keep returning to.

Three questions where your experience would change how we set up ablations:

1. How sensitive is ticket identification to the learning-rate warmup and total iteration budget during the pruning rounds? With shorter warmup, do tickets still transfer?
2. For structured sparsity (whole channels/blocks) instead of element-wise masks, did the same 10-20% threshold hold, or does it shift?
3. Your evaluations focus on MNIST/CIFAR-10. On harder or non-stationary targets, is the initialization lottery still the dominant factor, or does data order dominate?

Any pointers to follow-up work would help us avoid dead ends.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
