Уважаемый Umar Ahmed,

I am Yang Liu, a researcher working on LLM agents; my work is at https://foamliu.github.io/.

Your PIZZA study names a failure we have seen but never measured: a small fine-tuned parser emitting a well-formed wrong tree, with nothing downstream able to tell. The 300-run design (5 pool sizes × 5 seeds, hierarchical bootstrap, Holm correction fixed before the results were read) is the instrument we were missing.

Three questions:

1. WORDS reaches 87.5% exact match and cuts silent errors to 4.5% (JSON: 14.9%) while emitting 4.1× fewer tokens than EXR. Since both Qwen3.5-0.8B and LFM2.5-350M gain, how much do you attribute to reusing pretraining token statistics versus to lexical negation? Did you try a WORDS-style surface on a target without negation?

2. Negation is the largest residual category: 2.0% of orders for EXR versus 0.2% for WORDS. Would negation-only augmentation of the symbolic formats close part of that gap, or is the benefit tied to the surface form itself?

3. Refusing real orders costs 2.1–5.4%. How was the refusal threshold chosen, and does it stay fixed across pool sizes?

Thank you for the artifact and the appendix tables.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
