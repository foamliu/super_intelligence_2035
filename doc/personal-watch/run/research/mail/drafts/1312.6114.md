## 正文

Beste professor Welling,

I am Yang Liu, a researcher working on LLM agents; you can find my work at https://foamliu.github.io/. Your Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes paper is the reference I keep returning to while we design a latent-variable model for structured layout generation, and I would value your judgment on three points.

1. The reparameterized SGVB estimator assumes a continuous latent code. Our latent factors are closer to discrete choices (which layer a region is assigned to). Would you keep the latent continuous and discretize only downstream, or switch to a Gumbel/REINFORCE-style estimator at that point?
2. The recognition model amortizes inference across the dataset. On small in-domain datasets (~1e3 examples), have you seen amortization hurt relative to per-datapoint optimization, and is there a diagnostic beyond the ELBO that tells us it is happening?
3. For the KL term, do you have a practical weighting or warm-up schedule that keeps latents informative without sacrificing reconstruction in low-data regimes?

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
