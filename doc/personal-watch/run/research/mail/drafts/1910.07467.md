Subject: RMSNorm: p sensitivity and where the runtime gain comes from

Sehr geehrter Herr Professor Sennrich,

ich hoffe, diese Nachricht erreicht Sie gut.

My name is Yang Liu; I work on LLM agents and post-training recipes (https://foamliu.github.io/). RMSNorm is the normalization I keep coming back to when we budget compute for a small model, because the paper separates the cost of re-centering from the cost of rescaling rather than treating layer norm as one block.

Three questions:

1. For pRMSNorm with p% of the input used to estimate the RMS, is there a sensitivity table for p? I am trying to tell whether p=1/4 is a safe default or a choice tuned to the reported setup.

2. The claim that re-centering can be dropped was tested in a 2019 RNN/Transformer setting. Does it still hold with pre-LN plus long-sequence packing, where activations accumulate differently?

3. Of the reported 7%-64% runtime difference, how much comes from removing the mean subtraction versus operator fusion in your implementation? We need that split to decide whether norm deserves its own line in our cost model.

A short answer would already change how we set the norm cost term.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
