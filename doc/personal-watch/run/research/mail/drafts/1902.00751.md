## 正文

Dear Dr. Houlsby,

I am Yang Liu, a researcher working on LLM agents and parameter-efficient adaptation; you can find my work at https://foamliu.github.io/.

I keep returning to your Adapter paper because we need one frozen backbone to serve many task variants, and your 3.6% per-task parameter budget with a 0.4% GLUE gap is still the cleanest reference point for that trade-off.

Three questions:

1. When adapters are added incrementally for new tasks, can they be composed at inference without revisiting earlier tasks, or does interference force joint retraining once the task set grows?

2. How much of the result comes from adapter placement (after attention vs after FFN), bottleneck width, and the near-zero initialization? Which single factor would you fix first if compute allowed only one ablation?

3. For sequence-level generation (code or RTL) rather than classification, does a shared adapter pool still preserve the parameter-sharing benefit, or do output-side heads dominate the per-task budget?

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
