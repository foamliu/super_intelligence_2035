Sehr geehrter Herr Professor Riedel, ich schreibe auf Englisch, damit die technischen Details nicht durch meine Formulierungen verfälscht werden.

I am Yang Liu, a researcher working on LLM agents and pretraining; you can find my work at https://foamliu.github.io/.

RETRO is the paper we return to whenever someone proposes retrieval as a substitute for long context: 2048-token sequences cut into 64-token chunks, k neighbours retrieved with BERT keys of width r=128, a 600B-token training database against a 1.75T-token evaluation database, and Pile parity with 25x fewer parameters. We are pretraining a ~2B model on a mostly Chinese corpus and cannot yet tell whether the retrieval branch earns its serving cost. Three questions:

1. Between your 150M and 7B runs, did a crossover appear below which retrieval loses to simply adding training tokens? At roughly 2B parameters and 1T tokens, which side would you expect?
2. The 10 ms SCaNN lookup is amortized over 64 tokens; at interactive serving, how much index memory did the 1.75T-token database need, and does the lookup stay outside the latency budget?
3. Your decontamination uses 13-gram Jaccard at 0.8 with MinHash. Chinese reaches 13-gram overlap by chance more easily; would you tighten the threshold, or move to a tokenizer-aware n-gram?

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
