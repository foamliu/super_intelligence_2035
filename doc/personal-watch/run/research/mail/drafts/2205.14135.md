Dear Professor Dao,

I am Yang Liu (Foam), author of "Super Intelligence 2035" and lead of the
BaiZe-ISEDA2027 / ZhuLong-DAC2027 projects at the AI Research Institute of
ChangXin Memory Technologies. We pretrain a ~2B-parameter model and run
architecture ablations on EDA tasks.

Your FlashAttention paper changed how we read our own long-context slowdowns:
we had been counting FLOPs, and your IO-aware analysis explained why we were
stalling on memory traffic instead.

Two questions would directly shape our next configuration:

1) At 2k-8k sequence length and 1-3B parameters, when does the non-matmul
overhead (softmax, rescaling) start to outweigh the IO savings? Do you have a
rule of thumb for head_dim 64 vs 128, and for block size, on A100-class GPUs
(we are not on H100)?

2) Our ablation deltas are often around 0.01. Is the FA2 backward bitwise
deterministic across runs with a fixed seed? If not, what check would you
recommend to separate kernel noise from a real effect?

Thank you for keeping the kernels open - it is what lets small teams run these
experiments at all.

Best regards,
Yang Liu (Foam)
AI Research Institute, ChangXin Memory Technologies
