## 正文

Dear Dr. Zelikman,

My name is Yang Liu; I study LLM agents and post-training (https://foamliu.github.io/).

We are deciding whether a small model should spend extra computation on internal thoughts during continued pretraining, not only during task fine-tuning. Your zero-shot gains on GSM8K (5.9% to 10.9%) and CommonsenseQA (36.3% to 47.2%) are the cleanest evidence we have found that this can work without task supervision.

1. How expensive is tokenwise parallel sampling in practice? If every token may open a thought, what share of the forward-pass budget goes to thoughts, and did you cap thought length per token?
2. With only a REINFORCE-style signal on future-token likelihood, what keeps the model from writing thoughts that merely copy the next token? Did you need an explicit mixing weight between the thought-aware and base objectives?
3. Do the gains survive a much smaller continued-pretraining budget (tens of billions of tokens rather than hundreds), or do they only appear once the model has seen enough text to learn the thought format?

Thank you for the paper and for documenting the training details.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
