Subject: CFG — conditioning dropout rate and the guidance schedule

Geachte heer Salimans,

I am Yang Liu, a researcher working on LLM agents and generative modelling; you can find my work at https://foamliu.github.io/. Your note on classifier-free guidance is the reference we use whenever a diffusion stack has to trade sample fidelity against coverage without training an extra classifier.

Three questions:

1. The unconditional branch is obtained by dropping the condition during joint training. At which dropout probability did you settle, and is that number stable across datasets? We are training on a narrow industrial image domain and worry that a low drop rate leaves the unconditional score too close to the conditional one.

2. Do you apply one guidance weight for the whole trajectory, or does the useful weight drift with the noise level? For few-step distilled samplers the same weight may over-saturate early steps, and we would like to know whether you saw that.

3. Beyond visual inspection, is there a quantitative signal that tells you guidance is too high — colour saturation statistics, CLIP-score plateau, or FID turning over?

Concrete answers would change how we set up our diffusion baseline. Thank you.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
