## 主题

Gemini 1.5 long-context curves: three questions about the 10M deviation

## 正文

Dear Dr. Reid,

My name is Yang Liu; I work on LLM agents, and you can find my work at https://foamliu.github.io/.

I have been reading the Gemini 1.5 report while designing long-context evaluations for our own agent harness. Three details bear directly on a decision we are about to make.

1. In the NLL analysis the power law holds to 1M tokens for documents and about 2M for code, then deviates at 10M. Do you read that knee as a positional/attention-capacity limit, or as a change in corpus composition at those lengths? Is there one ablation that separates the two without full retraining?
2. Needle recall is already above 99.7% at 1M. For predicting downstream long-document QA in a much smaller model, which signal proved more informative: multi-needle recall, NLL at fixed token positions, or grounding to retrieved evidence?
3. Was long-context data introduced during pretraining or in a separate adaptation stage? If a 2B model can afford only one 128K extension phase, which would you choose first, and what would you log to catch a silent failure?

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
