Subject: Masked autoencoding in hindsight: three questions from a ~2B chip-design project

Dear Professor Xie,

I am Liu Yang, working on BaiZe-ISEDA2027 (a ~2B domain model for chip design) and ZhuLong-DAC2027 (an EDA agent harness). We are deciding how to pretrain a vision tower for semiconductor layout and waveform images.

Your MAE paper is the reference we keep returning to, and your recent "Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding" sharpened the question. Three points:

1. The 75% mask ratio was tuned on natural images. Our images have large uniform regions and very thin metal lines, so redundancy differs. Which measurable property of a dataset should set the mask ratio, and is structured (block or stripe) masking worth testing against random masking?

2. For dense downstream targets such as defect segmentation, how much does MAE's deliberately shallow decoder matter compared with encoder capacity? Would you preserve that asymmetry in a new domain?

3. In the unbundling paper, a jointly trained encoder matched a pretrained visual prior. With limited in-domain image-text pairs, should we still pretrain the vision tower with MAE first, or train it jointly from scratch with the backbone?

The answers would directly shape our pretraining pipeline and evaluation protocol. Thank you for considering these questions.

Best regards,
Liu Yang
Super Intelligence 2035 / BaiZe-ISEDA2027 / ZhuLong-DAC2027
