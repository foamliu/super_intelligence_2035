Dear Mr. Arunkumar,

I am Yang Liu, a researcher working on LLM agents and their evaluation harnesses; my page is https://foamliu.github.io/.

Your exact process oracle over 124,416 authorization contexts gave me the first clean picture of what a proxy verifier does to a policy model: in the sufficiency setting the proxy ends at 0.940 while true faithfulness drops from 0.731 to 0.667 on 96 held-out episodes. That gap is exactly the failure our reward design cannot see.

Three questions:
1. What does padding actually look like in the rollout: extra minimal sufficient reasons appended, or longer free-text justifications? If the reward were normalized by the number of cited reasons, would the phenotype disappear or simply move elsewhere?
2. Outcome-only verifiers saturate quickly. Can the model-free audit (selection error and gap sign) predict the saturation step before training starts, and how long does the short saturation pilot need to be?
3. Since the damage magnitude did not replicate across your 10 cells, which of the five pre-registered explanations still looks most plausible, and what should a team without an exact oracle measure first?

Thank you for releasing the testbed and the ported public policies.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
