## 正文

Dear Dr. Veličković,

I read your Graph Attention Networks paper closely while working on graph representations of circuit netlists, and I have two questions about extending the layer beyond its original setting.

Your layer scores e_ij = LeakyReLU(a^T [W h_i || W h_j]) and normalizes over neighbors, with K=8 heads concatenated. On netlists, edges carry strong categorical attributes (pin direction, net class, timing role) and node degrees are heavy-tailed because clock and reset nets touch almost everything. I am unsure whether the shared additive a(.,.) can express the edge-type interaction, or whether it mostly learns a degree-driven prior.

1. Did you observe the softmax over a very high-degree neighborhood washing out rare but critical edges? Is there a normalization or degree-aware trick you would try before resorting to edge-conditioned attention?
2. GATv2 later showed the scoring is static, i.e. the ranking of neighbors is query-independent. For heterogeneous netlists, does dynamic attention alone suffice, or would you keep a relation-specific W_r per edge type?
3. For graph-level heads, do you prefer mean or attention pooling over sum when high-degree hub nodes dominate?

Thank you for the paper and for any pointer.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
