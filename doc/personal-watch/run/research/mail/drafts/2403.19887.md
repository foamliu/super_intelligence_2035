## 正文

```
Dear Professor Belinkov,

I am Yang Liu, a researcher working on LLM agents; you can find my work at https://foamliu.github.io/.

Jamba is the only public report I know where the attention-to-Mamba ratio itself is a controlled variable: l = 8, a:m = 1:7, MoE every e = 2 layers, 52B total with 12B active, and 256K context. We are building a similar ablation table for long-context inference and want to keep our axes honest.

Three questions:

1. The paper says ablations ran at scales up to 7B. Which ratios were actually trained (1:1, 1:3, 1:7, pure Mamba), and did their perplexity ordering stay the same from 1.3B to 7B?

2. Holding active parameters at 12B, how much of the 256K-context quality survives without MoE? Is the long-context result attributable to the Mamba layers or to the extra capacity?

3. On retrieval-style probes between 128K and 256K tokens, do you see a systematic pattern in which layers (attention vs Mamba) carry the retrieved content?

Thank you for releasing the checkpoints and the ablation configurations.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
```
