1. In your ablations, did hard attention mainly sharpen localization, or also improve captions? We see much higher gradient variance and are unsure it is worth the cost.
2. Did you use attention entropy or the maps to diagnose whether the decoder reads the plot rather than the title and tick labels? Did any attention regularizer help?
3. With only tens of thousands of image-text pairs and a pretrained 2B vision-language backbone, would you still learn attention from scratch, or freeze the encoder and train only the attention and decoder?
