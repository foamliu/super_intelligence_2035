I am reading your DCGAN paper because we are training small vision encoders and image/video generators, and the architectural constraints you identified are still the default checklist. It is unclear to me which constraints remain load-bearing when the backbone changes.

Three specific questions:

1. In Section 4.1 you replaced pooling with strided convolutions. Which mattered more for avoiding mode collapse — strided convolutions or BatchNorm in both G and D? This decides what we keep when swapping the DCGAN generator for a ViT decoder.

2. When you removed fully connected hidden layers, did the 1×1 convolution at the 4×4 input matter more than a learned 4×4×512 projection for discriminator stability? We see divergence when we add a linear layer back.

3. Is smooth latent interpolation evidence of latent continuity, or can it hold even when some modes are dropped? That decides whether we can use it as an accept/reject metric.
