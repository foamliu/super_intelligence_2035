Dear Professor Grover,

I am Yang Liu, a researcher working on LLM agents; my work is at https://foamliu.github.io/.

I read your Decision Transformer paper because we are modelling multi-step tool-use trajectories as sequences instead of learning a critic. The framing - return-to-go, state and action tokens in one causal sequence, with no bootstrapping - is what made me try it.

Three questions, each would change a design decision:

1. Our returns are heavy-tailed. Would you condition on the maximum observed return, a percentile, or a clipped value? If the target is too optimistic, what fails first?

2. Episodes run far longer than the K transitions in context. Does the missing value bootstrapping hurt credit assignment there, or is a critic-based stage still needed?

3. Logged trajectories contain many near-tied alternative actions. Did that multimodality degrade performance, and did behaviour-cloning regularisation help beyond return conditioning?

Thank you for releasing the code and ablations; they made these questions concrete.

Best regards,
Yang Liu
