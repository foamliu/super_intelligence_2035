- 收件人：Umar Ahmed（ITMO University，一作）
- 发信时间：2026-11-16 03:03 · 回信时间：2026-11-17 21:20（+03，即 2026-11-18 02:20 +08） · 延迟：约 1.8 天
- 我们问的（原文摘要）：① WORDS 的收益有多少来自复用预训练 token 统计、多少来自词法化的否定；有没有在无否定目标上试过 WORDS 式表层；② 只对符号格式做否定增广能否缩小否定残差（EXR 2.0% vs WORDS 0.2%）；③ 拒识阈值怎么定、跨池是否固定。
- 对方答的（**英文原文照抄**）：

  > Your first one made us look at our data in a way we hadn’t yet, and it turned out to be very interesting.
  >
  > On negation vs. pre-training. We split the test orders by whether they contain a negation. Against DSL, its same-length twin, almost all of WORDS’s advantage comes from orders with a negation. So in that comparison, how “no” is written really is the mechanism. But against the native EXR format, WORDS still makes clearly fewer silent errors on orders without any negation, on both models and at every training size. So natural words help beyond negation too. We can’t yet say how much of that comes from pre-training familiarity, but it’s a good question to dig into.
  >
  > On negation-only augmentation. We haven’t tried it yet, but your question gave us a nice experiment: a DSL variant that spells only negation as “no”, and negation-oversampled training for the symbolic formats. One early hint: our TAGGED format already writes “no” inside key=value syntax, and it still has the most negation errors. That suggests the natural phrase matters, not just the word. We’ll happily share the results when we have them.
  >
  > On refusals. There’s no threshold. Refusal is simply a learned output: about 30% of each training set are non-orders labelled “?”, and decoding is greedy. That share was fixed in advance and is the same for every model and pool size; the actual refusal rate then varies a little with training size. The 2.1-5.4% you mention is the Qwen range; with LFM2 it becomes the 2.1-10.1% in the abstract.
  >
  > One small note: the numbers you quoted are for Qwen at the full training pool, and LFM2’s are a bit lower. And for WORDS, the most common remaining error is actually the number of items in an order, rather than negation.

- **可执行结论**：**表层自然语言的价值有两层** —— 对「同长度 DSL 孪生」，收益几乎全来自含否定的样本；但对原生 EXR，**无否定样本上仍显著更少静默错误** ⇒ 「自然词」本身（不只 `no` 这个词）在起作用，且 `TAGGED` 把 `no` 写进 key=value 仍是最差 ⇒ **不能靠符号化否定蒙混**。拒识**没有阈值**，是学出来的输出（训练集固定 30% 非指令标 `?`，贪心解码）。
- **归属线**：data（主）· harness（判分 / 拒识成本）· pretrain（指令微调配比）
- **建议采纳点**：
  1. `data`：工具调用 / 结构化输出若需否定语义（「不要把 A 和 B 放同一层」），**保留自然语言表层**，不要只做符号替换；对照实验至少三臂：DSL、DSL+自然否定、全自然语言。
  2. `harness`：静默错误要**按类别分层统计**（否定 / 数量 / 越界），别只报总 exact-match；本文作者提示**数量类才是最大残差**，我们造题时需单列「条目数」这一类。
  3. 拒识成本模型：**按模型给区间**（Qwen 2.1–5.4%，LFM2 2.1–10.1%），不要用单一阈值假设；若我们要做「不确定就拒识」，须预注册非指令样本比例（本文为 30%）。
  4. 引用数字时标注口径：我们引用的 87.5% / 4.5% / 14.9% 是 **Qwen 全训练池**，LFM2 更低 —— 对外报告必须带模型与池大小。
